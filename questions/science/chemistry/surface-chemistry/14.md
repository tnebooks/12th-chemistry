**Which one of the following is an example for homogeneous catalysis?**

```markdown
In homogeneous catalysis, the catalyst and the reactants are in the same phase. Hydrolysis of sucrose in presence of dilute HCl: both sucrose and HCl are in aqueous solution (same phase). Haber's process (Fe catalyst - solid, reactants gases) and Contact process (V₂O₅ - solid, gases) are heterogeneous catalysis. Hydrogenation of oil (Ni catalyst - solid, oil - liquid) is heterogeneous.
Correct answer is (d).
```
