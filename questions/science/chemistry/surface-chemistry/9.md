**The most effective electrolyte for the coagulation of As₂S₃ sol is**

```markdown
As₂S₃ sol is a negatively charged colloidal sol. According to Hardy-Schulze rule, the cation with higher valency has greater coagulating power. Among the given electrolytes: NaCl (Na⁺, +1), Ba(NO₃)₂ (Ba²⁺, +2), Al₂(SO₄)₃ (Al³⁺, +3), K₃[Fe(CN)₆] (K⁺, +1). Al³⁺ has the highest valency (+3), so Al₂(SO₄)₃ is the most effective coagulating electrolyte.
Correct answer is (d).
```
