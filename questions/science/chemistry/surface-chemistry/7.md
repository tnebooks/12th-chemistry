**Hair cream is**

```markdown
Hair cream is an emulsion. An emulsion is a colloidal dispersion where both the dispersed phase and dispersion medium are liquids. Hair cream consists of oil droplets dispersed in water (or water in oil), making it an emulsion.
Correct answer is (b) emulsion.
```
