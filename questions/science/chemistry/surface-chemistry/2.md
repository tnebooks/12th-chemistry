**Which of the following is incorrect for physisorption?**

```markdown
Physisorption is an exothermic process. Adsorption decreases with increase in temperature because the equilibrium shifts to the left (Le Chatelier's principle). Physisorption is reversible, has low heat of adsorption (20-40 kJ/mol), and increases with increase in surface area. Therefore, the statement "increases with increase in temperature" is incorrect.
Correct answer is (b).
```
