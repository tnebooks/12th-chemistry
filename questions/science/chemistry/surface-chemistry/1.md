**For Freundlich isotherm a graph of log(x/m) is plotted against log p. The slope of the line and its y-axis intercept respectively corresponds to**

```markdown
Freundlich adsorption isotherm equation: x/m = k × p^(1/n)
Taking log on both sides: log(x/m) = log k + (1/n) log p
Comparing with y = c + mx (straight line equation):
Slope (m) = 1/n and y-intercept (c) = log k
Correct answer is (c) 1/n, log k.
```
