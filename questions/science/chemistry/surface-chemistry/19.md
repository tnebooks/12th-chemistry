**On which of the following properties does the coagulating power of an ion depend? (NEET - 2018)**

```markdown
According to Hardy-Schulze rule, the coagulating power of an ion depends on both the magnitude (valency) and the sign of the charge on the ion. Higher valency means greater coagulating power. Also, ions with charge opposite to the colloidal sol are effective (sign matters). Therefore, both magnitude and sign of the charge determine coagulating power.
Correct answer is (a).
```
