**Match the following: A) V₂O₅, B) Ziegler-Natta, C) Peroxide, D) Finely divided Fe**

```markdown
V₂O₅ is used as catalyst in Contact process for H₂SO₄ (iv).
Ziegler-Natta catalyst is used for high density polyethylene (i).
Peroxide is used as initiator for PAN (polyacrylonitrile) (ii).
Finely divided Fe is used as catalyst in Haber's process for NH₃ (iii).
So A-iv, B-i, C-ii, D-iii.
Correct answer is (a).
```
