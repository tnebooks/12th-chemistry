choices:
  - "II > I > IV > III"
  - "I > II > III > IV"
  - "none of these"
answers:
  - "III > II > I > IV"
---

**In an electrical field, the particles of a colloidal system move towards cathode. The coagulation of the same sol is studied using K₂SO₄(i), Na₃PO₄(ii), K₄[Fe(CN)₆](iii) and NaCl (iv). Their coagulating power should be**

```markdown
The particles move towards cathode, so the sol is positively charged. For coagulation of positively charged sol, the anion with higher valency has greater coagulating power. The anions: K₂SO₄ → SO₄²⁻ (valency 2), Na₃PO₄ → PO₄³⁻ (valency 3), K₄[Fe(CN)₆] → [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻ (valency 4), NaCl → Cl⁻ (valency 1). Higher valency = greater coagulating power. Order: [Fe(CN)₆]⁴⁻ > PO₄³⁻ > SO₄²⁻ > Cl⁻. So III > II > I > IV.
Correct answer is (b).
```
