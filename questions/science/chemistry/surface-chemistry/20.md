**Match the following: A) Pure nitrogen, B) Haber process, C) Contact process, D) Deacon's process**

```markdown
Pure nitrogen is obtained from sodium azide (or barium azide) (iv).
Haber process is used for manufacture of ammonia (NH₃) (iii).
Contact process is used for manufacture of sulphuric acid (H₂SO₄) (ii).
Deacon's process is used for manufacture of chlorine (Cl₂) (i).
So A-iv, B-iii, C-ii, D-i.
Correct answer is (d).
```
