**Adsorption of a gas on solid metal surface is spontaneous and exothermic, then**

```markdown
For spontaneous process: ΔG is negative. For exothermic process: ΔH is negative. When gas molecules adsorb on a solid surface, they lose their freedom of movement (become more ordered), so entropy decreases (ΔS is negative). Therefore, ΔS decreases.
Correct answer is (d) ΔS decreases.
```
