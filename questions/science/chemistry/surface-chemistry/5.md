**Assertion: Coagulation power of Al³⁺ is more than Na⁺. Reason: greater the valency of the flocculating ion added, greater is its power to cause precipitation**

```markdown
According to Hardy-Schulze rule, the coagulating power of an ion increases with increase in its valency (charge). Al³⁺ has +3 charge while Na⁺ has +1 charge. Therefore, Al³⁺ has greater coagulating power than Na⁺. Both assertion and reason are true and reason correctly explains the assertion.
Correct answer is (a).
```
