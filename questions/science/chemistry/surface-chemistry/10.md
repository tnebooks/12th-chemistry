**Which one of the following is not a surfactant?**

```markdown
Surfactants (surface active agents) have a hydrophilic head and a hydrophobic tail. CH₃—(CH₂)₁₅—NH₂ (hexadecylamine) does not have a charged or strongly polar head group that makes it a good surfactant. The other options have charged or polar head groups (quaternary ammonium, sulfate, carboxylate) and are surfactants.
Correct answer is (b).
```
