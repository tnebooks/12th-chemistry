**To stop bleeding from an injury, ferric chloride can be applied. Which comment about the statement is justified?**

```markdown
Blood is a negatively charged colloidal sol. When FeCl₃ is applied, Fe³⁺ ions (with high positive charge) neutralize the negative charge on blood particles and cause coagulation (clotting). This stops bleeding. Therefore, the statement is true because Fe³⁺ ions coagulate blood which is a negatively charged sol.
Correct answer is (b).
```
