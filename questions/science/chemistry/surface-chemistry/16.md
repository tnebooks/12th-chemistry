**The coagulation values in millimoles per litre of the electrolytes used for the coagulation of As₂S₃ are given below: (I) NaCl = 52, (II) BaCl₂ = 0.69, (III) MgSO₄ = 0.22. The correct order of their coagulating power is**

```markdown
Coagulating power is inversely proportional to coagulation value (smaller the coagulation value, greater the coagulating power).
Coagulation values: NaCl = 52, BaCl₂ = 0.69, MgSO₄ = 0.22.
Ascending order of coagulation values: 0.22 (MgSO₄) < 0.69 (BaCl₂) < 52 (NaCl).
So coagulating power: MgSO₄ > BaCl₂ > NaCl i.e., III > II > I.
Correct answer is (a).
```
