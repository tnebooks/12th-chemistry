answers:
  - "x/m = PT"

**If x is the amount of adsorbate and m is the amount of adsorbent, which of the following relations is not related to adsorption process?**

```markdown
Adsorption depends on pressure (P) at constant temperature: x/m = f(P). It depends on temperature (T) at constant pressure: x/m = f(T). It also depends on both P and T. However, x/m = PT is not a correct relation for adsorption. The correct relation is x/m = k × P^(1/n) (Freundlich isotherm).
Correct answer is (d) x/m = PT.
```
