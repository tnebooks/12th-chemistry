**A solid compound XY has NaCl structure. if the radius of the cation is 100 pm, the radius of the anion will be**

```markdown
In NaCl structure (octahedral), the limiting radius ratio r+/r- = 0.414. Given r+ = 100 pm, 100/r- = 0.414 → r- = 100/0.414. Correct answer is (a) (100/0.414).
```
