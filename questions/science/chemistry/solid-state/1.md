**Graphite and diamond are**

```markdown
Graphite and diamond are both allotropes of carbon. In both, carbon atoms are bonded by covalent bonds. Graphite has a layered structure while diamond has a three-dimensional network structure. Both are covalent crystals (network solids). Therefore, the correct answer is (c) Both are covalent crystals.
```
