**The yellow colour in NaCl crystal is due to**

```markdown
When NaCl crystals are heated in sodium vapour, Cl⁻ ions escape from the lattice and electrons get trapped at the vacancies. These trapped electrons form F-centres (Farbenzentren). These electrons absorb visible light and get excited, causing the crystal to appear yellow. Correct answer is (a) excitation of electrons in F centers.
```
