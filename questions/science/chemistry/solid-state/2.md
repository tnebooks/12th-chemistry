**An ionic compound AₓBᵧ crystallizes in fcc type crystal structure with B ions at the centre of each face and A ion occupying corners of the cube. The correct formula of AₓBᵧ is**

```markdown
In fcc structure: Number of A ions at corners = 8 × (1/8) = 1. Number of B ions at face centers = 6 × (1/2) = 3. Therefore A : B = 1 : 3. The formula is AB₃. Correct answer is (b) AB₃.
```
