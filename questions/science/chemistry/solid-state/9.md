**The ionic radii of A⁺ and B⁻ are 0.98 × 10⁻¹⁰ m and 1.81 × 10⁻¹⁰ m. the coordination number of each ion in AB is**

```markdown
Radius ratio (r+/r-) = 0.98/1.81 = 0.541. This value lies between 0.414 and 0.732, which corresponds to octahedral geometry. In octahedral geometry, coordination number is 6. Correct answer is (c) 6.
```
