**Schottky defect in a crystal is observed when**

```markdown
Schottky defect occurs when equal numbers of cations and anions are missing from their regular lattice sites (vacancies). This results in a decrease in density of the crystal. It is commonly found in NaCl, KCl, etc. Correct answer is (b).
```
