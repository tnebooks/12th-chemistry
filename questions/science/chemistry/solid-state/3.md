**The ratio of close packed atoms to tetrahedral hole in cubic packing is**

```markdown
In cubic close packing (ccp/fcc), if there are N atoms, there are 2N tetrahedral voids and N octahedral voids. Therefore, the ratio of close packed atoms to tetrahedral voids = N : 2N = 1 : 2. Correct answer is (b) 1:2.
```
