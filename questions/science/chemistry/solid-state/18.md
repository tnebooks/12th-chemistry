**Potassium has a bcc structure with nearest neighbor distance 4.52 Å. Its atomic weight is 39. Its density will be**

```markdown
For bcc, a√3/2 = 4.52 Å → a = 4.52 × 2/√3 = 5.22 Å = 5.22 × 10⁻¹⁰ m. bcc has Z = 2 atoms per unit cell. Density ρ = (Z × M)/(Nₐ × a³) = (2 × 39 × 10⁻³)/(6.023 × 10²³ × (5.22 × 10⁻¹⁰)³) = 78/(6.023 × 10²³ × 1.422 × 10⁻²⁸) = 78/(8.565 × 10⁻⁵) = 910 kg m⁻³ ≈ 915 kg m⁻³. Correct answer is (a) 915 kg m⁻³.
```
