**The cation leaves its normal position in the crystal and moves to some interstitial position, the defect in the crystal is known as**

```markdown
When a cation (usually a smaller ion) leaves its regular lattice site and occupies an interstitial site, a vacancy is created at the original site and an interstitial defect is created. This is called Frenkel defect. It is commonly found in ZnO, AgCl, AgBr, etc. Correct answer is (c) Frenkel defect.
```
