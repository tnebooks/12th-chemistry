**The crystal with a metal deficiency defect is**

```markdown
FeO (iron(II) oxide) shows metal deficiency defect (non-stoichiometric defect). In FeO, some Fe²⁺ ions are missing and to maintain electrical neutrality, some Fe³⁺ ions are present. This gives the formula Fe₀.₉₅O. ZnO shows Frenkel defect. NaCl and KCl show Schottky defect. Correct answer is (b) FeO.
```
