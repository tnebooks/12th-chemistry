**Assertion: due to Frenkel defect, density of the crystalline solid decreases. Reason: in Frenkel defect cation and anion leaves the crystal.**

```markdown
Assertion is false. In Frenkel defect, ions do not leave the crystal; they simply move to interstitial sites. Therefore, density remains unchanged. Only Schottky defect decreases density. Reason is also false because cations and anions do not leave the crystal in Frenkel defect. Both assertion and reason are false. Correct answer is (d).
```
