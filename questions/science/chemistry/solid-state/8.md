**In a solid atom M occupies ccp lattice and (1/3) of tetrahedral voids are occupied by atom N. Find the formula of solid formed by M and N.**

```markdown
In ccp, number of M atoms = N (4 per unit cell). Number of tetrahedral voids = 2N. N atoms occupy 1/3 of tetrahedral voids = (1/3) × 2N = 2N/3. M : N = N : (2N/3) = 3 : 2. Formula is M₃N₂. Correct answer is (d) M₃N₂.
```
