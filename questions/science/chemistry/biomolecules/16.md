**Which of the following vitamins is water soluble?**

```markdown
Vitamin B (B-complex vitamins) and Vitamin C are water-soluble vitamins. Vitamins A, D, E, and K are fat-soluble vitamins. Therefore, the correct answer is Vitamin B.
```
