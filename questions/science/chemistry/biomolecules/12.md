**The number of sp$^2$ and sp$^3$ hybridised carbon in fructose are respectively**

```markdown
Fructose has one carbonyl carbon (C=O) which is sp² hybridized and five other carbons that are sp³ hybridized. Therefore, the number of sp² and sp³ hybridized carbons in fructose are 1 and 5 respectively.
```
