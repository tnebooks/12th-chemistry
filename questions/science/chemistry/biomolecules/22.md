**α-D(+) Glucose and β-D(+) glucose are**

```markdown
α-D(+) Glucose and β-D(+) Glucose differ only in the configuration at the anomeric carbon (C₁). Such isomers are called anomers. Therefore, the correct answer is Anomers.
```
