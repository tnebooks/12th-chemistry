**In a protein, various amino acids linked together by (NEET Phase – I)**

```markdown
Amino acids in a protein are linked together by peptide bonds (amide linkages) formed between the carboxyl group of one amino acid and the amino group of another. Therefore, the correct answer is Peptide bond.
```
