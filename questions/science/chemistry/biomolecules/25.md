**Which of the following amino acids are achiral?**

```markdown
Glycine (NH₂-CH₂-COOH) has no chiral center because it has two hydrogen atoms attached to the α-carbon. It is the only achiral amino acid. Alanine, Leucine, and Proline are chiral. Therefore, the correct answer is Glycine.
```
