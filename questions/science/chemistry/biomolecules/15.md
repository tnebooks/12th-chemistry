**The secondary structure of a protein refers to**

```markdown
The secondary structure of a protein refers to the regular folding of the polypeptide backbone into structures such as α-helix and β-pleated sheet. The sequence of amino acids is the primary structure, and hydrophobic interactions contribute to tertiary structure. Therefore, the correct answer is α-helical backbone.
```
