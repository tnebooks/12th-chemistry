**In aqueous solution of amino acids mostly exists in,**

```markdown
In aqueous solution, amino acids exist as zwitterions (dipolar ions) with both positive and negative charges: H₃N⁺-CH(R)-COO⁻. The amino group is protonated and the carboxyl group is deprotonated. Therefore, the correct answer is H₃N⁺-CH(R)-COO⁻.
```
