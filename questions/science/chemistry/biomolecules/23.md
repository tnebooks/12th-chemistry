**Which of the following are epimers**

```markdown
Epimers are sugars that differ in configuration at only one chiral carbon. D-Glucose and D-Galactose differ at C₄, and D-Glucose and D-Mannose differ at C₂. Therefore, both pairs are epimers. The correct answer is Both (a) and (b).
```
