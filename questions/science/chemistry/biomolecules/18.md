**Which of the following statement is not correct?**

```markdown
Denaturation of proteins leads to loss of their biological activity, not activation. Ovalbumin is a food reserve protein in egg-white, thrombin and fibrinogen are involved in blood clotting, and insulin maintains blood sugar levels. Therefore, the incorrect statement is that denaturation makes protein more active.
```
