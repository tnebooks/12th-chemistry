**Which one of the following is not produced by body?**

```markdown
Vitamins are essential nutrients that cannot be synthesized by the human body in sufficient amounts and must be obtained from diet. DNA, enzymes, and hormones are all produced by the body. Therefore, the correct answer is Vitamins.
```
