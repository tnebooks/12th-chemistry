**Complete hydrolysis of cellulose gives**

```markdown
Cellulose is a polymer of β-D-glucose units linked by β-1,4-glycosidic bonds. On complete hydrolysis, it gives D-Glucose. Therefore, the correct answer is D-Glucose.
```
