**Glucose $\xrightarrow{(HCN)}$ Product $\xrightarrow{(hydrolysis)}$ Product $\xrightarrow{(HI + Heat)}$ A, the compound A is**

```markdown
Glucose reacts with HCN to form cyanohydrin. On hydrolysis, it gives a hydroxy acid. On reduction with HI and heat, it gives heptanoic acid. The chain length increases by one carbon atom. Therefore, the correct answer is Heptanoic acid.
```
