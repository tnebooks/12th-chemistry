**The pyrimidine bases present in DNA are**

```markdown
The pyrimidine bases present in DNA are Cytosine (C) and Thymine (T). Adenine and Guanine are purine bases. Uracil is present in RNA instead of Thymine. Therefore, the correct answer is Cytosine and Thiamine.
```
