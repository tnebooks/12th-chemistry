**Assertion: A solution of sucrose in water is dextrorotatory. But on hydrolysis in the presence of little hydrochloric acid, it becomes levorotatory. (AIIMS)**
**Reason: Sucrose hydrolysis gives equal amounts of glucose and fructose. As a result of this change in sign of rotation is observed.**

```markdown
Both assertion and reason are true. Sucrose is dextrorotatory. On hydrolysis, it gives equimolar amounts of D(+) glucose and D(-) fructose. The resulting mixture is laevorotatory because the levorotation of fructose (-92°) is greater than the dextrorotation of glucose (+52.5°). Therefore, the reason correctly explains the assertion.
```
