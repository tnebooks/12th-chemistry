**An alcohol (X) gives blue colour in Victor Meyer's test and 3.7g of X when treated with metallic sodium liberates 560 mL of hydrogen at 273 K and 1 atm pressure. What will be the possible structure of X?**

```markdown
2ROH + 2Na → 2RONa + H₂
1 mole H₂ (22.4 L at STP) is produced from 2 moles of alcohol.
560 mL = 0.56 L → moles of H₂ = 0.56/22.4 = 0.025 mol
Moles of alcohol = 2 × 0.025 = 0.05 mol
Molar mass = 3.7/0.05 = 74 g mol⁻¹
For alcohol CₙH₂ₙ₊₁OH: 14n + 18 = 74 → 14n = 56 → n = 4
The alcohol gives blue colour in Victor Meyer's test, so it is a secondary alcohol.
Secondary alcohol with 4 carbons: CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₃ (butan-2-ol).
Correct answer is (a) CH₃CH(OH)CH₂CH₃.
```
