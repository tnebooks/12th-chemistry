**On reacting with neutral ferric chloride, phenol gives**

```markdown
Phenol reacts with neutral ferric chloride (FeCl₃) to give a violet colour. This is a characteristic test for phenols. The violet colour is due to the formation of ferric phenoxide complex.
C₆H₅OH + FeCl₃ → [Fe(OC₆H₅)₆]³⁻ + 3HCl
(violet colour)
Correct answer is (b) violet colour.
```
