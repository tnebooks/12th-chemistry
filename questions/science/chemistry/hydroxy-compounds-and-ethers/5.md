**Which one of the following is the strongest acid?**

```markdown
Electron withdrawing groups (NO₂, Cl) increase the acidity of phenols by stabilizing the phenoxide ion.
Among nitro phenols, para-nitrophenol (4-nitrophenol) is the strongest acid because the nitro group at para position has strong -I and -R effects, stabilizing the phenoxide ion most effectively. 2-nitrophenol has intramolecular hydrogen bonding which reduces acidity. 3-nitrophenol has only -I effect. 4-chlorophenol is weaker than nitrophenols.
Correct answer is (c) 4-nitrophenol.
```
