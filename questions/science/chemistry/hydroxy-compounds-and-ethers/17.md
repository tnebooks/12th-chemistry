**The reaction: RONa + R'X → ROR' + NaX is called**

```markdown
The reaction of sodium alkoxide with alkyl halide to form ether is called Williamson ether synthesis (or Williamson reaction).
RONa + R'X → ROR' + NaX
It proceeds via SN² mechanism.
Correct answer is (c) Williamson reaction.
```
