**Ethanol when heated with conc. H₂SO₄ at 170°C gives ethene. Ethene on reaction with H₂SO₄/H₂O gives ethanol. This is**

```markdown
Ethanol on dehydration with conc. H₂SO₄ at 170°C gives ethene:
CH₃CH₂OH → CH₂=CH₂ + H₂O
Ethene on hydration with H₂SO₄/H₂O gives ethanol back:
CH₂=CH₂ + H₂O → CH₃CH₂OH
This is an example of reversible reaction and hydration.
Correct answer is (a) CH₃CH₂OH.
```
