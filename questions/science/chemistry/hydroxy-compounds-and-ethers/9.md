**Propene reacts with H₂SO₄/H₂O to give**

```markdown
Propene (CH₃CH=CH₂) on hydration with H₂SO₄/H₂O follows Markovnikov's rule. The OH group attaches to the more substituted carbon, giving propan-2-ol (isopropyl alcohol): CH₃CH(OH)CH₃.
Correct answer is (b) CH₃CH(OH)CH₃.
```
