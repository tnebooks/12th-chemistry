**Williamson synthesis of preparing dimethyl ether is a/an**

```markdown
Williamson synthesis: CH₃ONa + CH₃I → CH₃OCH₃ + NaI
This reaction proceeds via SN² mechanism because the nucleophile (CH₃O⁻) attacks the carbon from the back side and the leaving group (I⁻) leaves simultaneously.
Correct answer is (b) SN² reaction.
```
