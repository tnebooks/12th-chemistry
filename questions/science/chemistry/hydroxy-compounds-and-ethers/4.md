---
choices:
  - "Chloroethane and NaOH"
  - "Ethanol and H₂SO₄"
  - "Ethanol and H₂O"
answers:
  - "2-chloroethanol and NaHCO₃"
---

**In the reaction sequence: Ethene → A → ethane-1,2-diol. A and X respectively are**

```markdown
Ethene reacts with HOCl (hypochlorous acid) to give 2-chloroethanol (A):
CH₂=CH₂ + HOCl → CH₂(OH)CH₂Cl
This on treatment with NaHCO₃ gives ethane-1,2-diol:
CH₂(OH)CH₂Cl + NaHCO₃ → CH₂(OH)CH₂OH
Correct answer is (c) 2-chloroethanol and NaHCO₃.
```
