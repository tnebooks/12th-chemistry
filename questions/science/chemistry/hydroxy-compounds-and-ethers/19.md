**Among the following ethers which one will produce methyl alcohol on treatment with hot HI?**

```markdown
When an ether is treated with hot HI, the bond between O and the more substituted carbon breaks via SN¹ mechanism (tertiary alkyl group gives more stable carbocation).
For (CH₃)₃C-O-CH₃, the C-O bond at the tert-butyl side breaks to give (CH₃)₃C-I and methanol (CH₃OH).
Correct answer is (a) (CH₃)₃C-O-CH₃.
```
