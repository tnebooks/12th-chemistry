**Which of the following compound can be used as antifreeze in automobile radiators?**

```markdown
Ethane-1,2-diol (ethylene glycol) is used as an antifreeze in automobile radiators. It lowers the freezing point of water and raises its boiling point. Methanol and ethanol are not suitable due to their volatility and lower efficiency. Neopentyl alcohol is a solid at room temperature.
Correct answer is (d) ethane-1,2-diol.
```
