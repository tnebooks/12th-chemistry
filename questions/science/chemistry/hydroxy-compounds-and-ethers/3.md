  - "CH₃CH₂CH(OH)CH₂CH₃"

answers:
  - "CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂CH₂OH"
---

**Hydroboration-oxidation of pent-1-ene gives**

```markdown
Hydroboration-oxidation follows anti-Markovnikov addition.
For pent-1-ene (CH₃CH₂CH₂CH=CH₂), BH₃ adds BH₂ to the terminal carbon (less substituted) and H to the internal carbon.
After oxidation, the alcohol is formed at the terminal carbon: CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂CH₂OH (pentan-1-ol).
Correct answer is (a) CH₃CH₂CH₂CH₂CH₂OH.
```
