**Isopropylbenzene on air oxidation in the presence of dilute acid gives**

```markdown
Isopropylbenzene (cumene) on air oxidation followed by acid hydrolysis gives phenol (C₆H₅OH) and acetone. This is called the Cumene process for the industrial preparation of phenol.
C₆H₅CH(CH₃)₂ + O₂ → C₆H₅C(CH₃)₂OOH → C₆H₅OH + CH₃COCH₃
Correct answer is (d) C₆H₅OH.
```
