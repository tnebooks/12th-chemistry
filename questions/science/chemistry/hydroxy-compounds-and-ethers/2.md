**Which of the following compounds on reaction with methyl magnesium bromide will give tertiary alcohol?**

```markdown
Methyl magnesium bromide (CH₃MgBr) reacts with esters to give tertiary alcohols.
Methyl propanoate (CH₃CH₂COOCH₃) + 2 CH₃MgBr → CH₃CH₂C(OH)(CH₃)₂ (tertiary alcohol).
Benzaldehyde gives secondary alcohol, acetaldehyde gives secondary alcohol, propanoic acid gives ketone (not alcohol).
Correct answer is (c) methyl propanoate.
```
