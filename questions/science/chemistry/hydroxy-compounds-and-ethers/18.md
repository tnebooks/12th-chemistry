**One mole of an organic compound (A) with formula C₃H₈O reacts completely with two moles of HI to form X and Y. When Y is boiled with aqueous alkali it forms Z. Z answers the iodoform test. The compound (A) is**

```markdown
C₃H₈O with two moles of HI gives CH₃I (X) and CH₃CH₂I (Y).
Y (CH₃CH₂I) on boiling with aqueous alkali gives ethanol (Z).
Ethanol gives iodoform test.
So the compound is methoxy ethane (CH₃OCH₂CH₃).
Correct answer is (d) methoxy ethane.
```
