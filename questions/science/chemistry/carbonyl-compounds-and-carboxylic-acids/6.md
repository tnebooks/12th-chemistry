**Predict the product Z in the following series of reactions: Ethanoic acid → PCl₅ → X → C₆H₆, Anhydrous AlCl₃ → Y → 10CH₃MgBr / 10H₂O → Z**

```markdown
Ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH) + PCl₅ → CH₃COCl (acetyl chloride) (X)
CH₃COCl + C₆H₆ (Friedel-Crafts acylation) → C₆H₅COCH₃ (acetophenone) (Y)
Acetophenone + 2CH₃MgBr → (CH₃)₂C(OH)C₆H₅ (tertiary alcohol) (Z)
Correct answer is (a) (CH₃)₂C(OH)C₆H₅.
```
