**In the following reaction, HC≡CH → H₂SO₄ → X. Product 'X' will not give**

```markdown
Acetylene (HC≡CH) on hydration with H₂SO₄/HgSO₄ gives acetaldehyde (CH₃CHO). Acetaldehyde gives Tollens test (silver mirror), Fehling solution test (red precipitate), and iodoform test (yellow precipitate). Victor Meyer test is used to distinguish primary, secondary and tertiary alcohols, not for aldehydes. So acetaldehyde will not give Victor Meyer test.
Correct answer is (b) Victor Meyer test.
```
