**The formation of cyanohydrin from acetone is an example of**

```markdown
Acetone reacts with HCN to form acetone cyanohydrin. In this reaction, the cyanide ion (CN⁻) acts as a nucleophile and attacks the carbonyl carbon of acetone. This is followed by protonation to give cyanohydrin. Since the nucleophile adds to the carbonyl group, it is a nucleophilic addition reaction.
Correct answer is (d) Nucleophilic addition.
```
