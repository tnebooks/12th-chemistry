```markdown
HVZ (Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky) reaction requires an α-hydrogen atom in the carboxylic acid. 2,2-dimethyl propanoic acid [CH₃-C(CH₃)₂-COOH] has no α-hydrogen because the α-carbon is fully substituted with methyl groups. Both assertion and reason are true and reason correctly explains the assertion.
```
