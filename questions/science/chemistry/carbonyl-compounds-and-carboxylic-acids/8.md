**Which of the following represents the correct order of acidity in the given compounds?**

```markdown
Electronegativity order: F > Cl > Br > H. More electronegative substituent increases the acidity by -I effect. So FCH₂COOH is strongest, followed by ClCH₂COOH, then BrCH₂COOH. CH₃COOH has no electron withdrawing group, so it is the weakest. Correct order: FCH₂COOH > ClCH₂COOH > BrCH₂COOH > CH₃COOH.
Correct answer is (b).
```
