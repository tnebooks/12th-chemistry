**Which one of the following undergoes reaction with 50% sodium hydroxide solution to give the corresponding alcohol and acid?**

```markdown
Phenylmethanal (benzaldehyde) has no α-hydrogen. It undergoes Cannizzaro reaction with 50% NaOH to give benzyl alcohol and sodium benzoate. Ethanal, propanal have α-hydrogen and undergo aldol condensation, not Cannizzaro.
Correct answer is (a) Phenylmethanal.
```
