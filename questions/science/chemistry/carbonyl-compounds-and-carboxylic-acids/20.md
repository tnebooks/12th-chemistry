**The reagent used to distinguish between acetaldehyde and benzaldehyde is**

```markdown
Acetaldehyde (aliphatic aldehyde) reduces Fehling's solution (red precipitate), while benzaldehyde (aromatic aldehyde) does not. Both give Tollens test, 2,4-DNP test, and semicarbazide test (since both are aldehydes). So Fehling's solution is used to distinguish them.
Correct answer is (b) Fehling's solution.
```
