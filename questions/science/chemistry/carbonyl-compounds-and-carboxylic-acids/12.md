**Which one of the following reduces Tollens reagent?**

```markdown
Tollens reagent (ammoniacal silver nitrate) is reduced by aldehydes. Formic acid (HCOOH) also reduces Tollens reagent because it has an aldehydic H atom and acts as a reducing agent. Acetic acid and benzophenone do not reduce Tollens reagent.
Correct answer is (a) formic acid.
```
