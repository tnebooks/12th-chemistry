**Ethanoic acid → P/Br₂ → 2-bromoethanoic acid. This reaction is called**

```markdown
The reaction of carboxylic acids with P/Br₂ (or PCl₅/Cl₂) to introduce halogen at the α-position is called the Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (HVZ) reaction. Ethanoic acid (CH₃COOH) on HVZ reaction gives 2-bromoethanoic acid (BrCH₂COOH).
Correct answer is (c) Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky reaction.
```
