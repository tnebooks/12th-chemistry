**CH₂=CH₂ → i) O₃ ii) Zn/H₂O → X**

```markdown
Ethene on ozonolysis (O₃ followed by Zn/H₂O) gives formaldehyde (HCHO). The double bond is cleaved and each carbon forms a carbonyl group. For ethene, both carbons give formaldehyde.
Correct answer is (a) Formaldehyde.
```
