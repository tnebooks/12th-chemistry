**An alkene "A" on reaction with O₃ and Zn-H₂O gives propanone and ethanol in equimolar ratio. Addition of HCl to alkene "A" gives "B" as the major product. The structure of product "B" is**

```markdown
Ozonolysis gives propanone (CH₃COCH₃) and ethanol (CH₃CHO). So the alkene is CH₃CH=C(CH₃)₂ (2-methylbut-2-ene). Addition of HCl to this alkene gives the more substituted alkyl chloride (Markovnikov addition): CH₃CH₂-C(Cl)(CH₃)₂? Actually the product is CH₃CH₂-CH(Cl)-CH₃. 
Correct answer is (b) H₃C-CH₂-CH(Cl)-CH₃.
```
