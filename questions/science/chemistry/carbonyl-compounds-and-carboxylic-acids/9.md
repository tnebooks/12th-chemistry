answers:

**Benzoic acid → 10NH₃ → A → NaOBr → B → NaNO₂/HCl → C. 'C' is**

```markdown
Benzoic acid (C₆H₅COOH) + NH₃ → C₆H₅CONH₂ (benzamide) (A)
Benzamide + NaOBr (Hofmann bromamide degradation) → C₆H₅NH₂ (aniline) (B)
Aniline + NaNO₂/HCl (diazotization) → C₆H₅N₂⁺Cl⁻ (benzene diazonium chloride) (C)
Correct answer is (c) benzene diazonium chloride.
```
