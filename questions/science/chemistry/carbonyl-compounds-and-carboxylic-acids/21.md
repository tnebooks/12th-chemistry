**Phenyl methanal is reacted with concentrated NaOH to give two products X and Y. X reacts with metallic sodium to liberate hydrogen. X and Y are**

```markdown
Phenyl methanal (benzaldehyde) undergoes Cannizzaro reaction with concentrated NaOH to give phenyl methanol (benzyl alcohol) (X) and sodium benzoate (Y). X (benzyl alcohol) reacts with Na to liberate H₂. So X = phenyl methanol, Y = sodium benzoate.
Correct answer is (c) phenyl methanol and sodium benzoate.
```
