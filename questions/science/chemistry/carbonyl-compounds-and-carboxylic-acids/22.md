**In which of the following reactions new carbon-carbon bond is not formed?**

```markdown
Aldol condensation forms C-C bond between two carbonyl compounds. Friedel-Crafts reaction forms C-C bond between benzene and alkyl/acyl group. Kolbe's reaction forms C-C bond between two phenoxide ions. Wolf-Kishner reduction reduces carbonyl group to methylene group without forming new C-C bond.
Correct answer is (d) Wolf Kishner reduction.
```
