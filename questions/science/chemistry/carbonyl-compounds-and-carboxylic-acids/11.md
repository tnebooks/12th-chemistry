**CH₃Br → KCN → (A) → H₂O⁺ → (B) → PCl₅ → (C). Product (C) is**

```markdown
CH₃Br + KCN → CH₃CN (methyl cyanide) (A)
CH₃CN + H₂O⁺ → CH₃COOH (acetic acid) (B)
CH₃COOH + PCl₅ → CH₃COCl (acetyl chloride) (C)
Correct answer is (a) acetyl chloride.
```
