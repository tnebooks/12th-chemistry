**Reaction of acetone with one of the following reagents involves nucleophilic addition followed by elimination of water. The reagent is**

```markdown
Acetone reacts with hydrazine (NH₂NH₂) in the presence of slightly acidic solution to form hydrazone. This reaction involves nucleophilic addition of hydrazine to the carbonyl group followed by elimination of water to form C=N bond. This is the formation of hydrazone.
Correct answer is (c) hydrazine in presence of slightly acidic solution.
```
