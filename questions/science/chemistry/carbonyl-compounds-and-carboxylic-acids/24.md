**Carboxylic acids have higher boiling points than aldehydes, ketones and even alcohols of comparable molecular mass. It is due to their ______ (NEET)**

```markdown
Carboxylic acids have higher boiling points because they form strong intermolecular hydrogen bonding (dimer formation) through two O-H bonds. This leads to extensive association and higher boiling points. Alcohols also have H-bonding but carboxylic acids have stronger and more extensive association.
Correct answer is (d) formation of intermolecular H-bonding.
```
