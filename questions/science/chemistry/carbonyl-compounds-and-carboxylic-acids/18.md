**Which one of the following reaction is an example of disproportionation reaction?**

```markdown
Cannizzaro reaction is a disproportionation reaction where an aldehyde without α-hydrogen undergoes simultaneous oxidation and reduction in presence of concentrated alkali. One molecule is reduced to alcohol and another is oxidized to carboxylic acid.
Correct answer is (b) Cannizzaro reaction.
```
