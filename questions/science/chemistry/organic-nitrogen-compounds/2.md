**The method by which aniline cannot be prepared is**

```markdown
Aniline can be prepared by Hofmann bromamide degradation (benzamide with Br₂/NaOH), reduction of nitrobenzene with Sn/HCl, and reduction of nitrobenzene with LiAlH₄. However, aniline cannot be prepared by Gabriel phthalimide synthesis using chlorobenzene because aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution with potassium phthalimide. Therefore, the correct answer is option (b).
```
