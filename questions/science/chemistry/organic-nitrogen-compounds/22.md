**Among the following, the reaction that proceeds through an electrophilic substitution is: d**

```markdown
Electrophilic substitution reactions (like chlorination of benzene) require a Lewis acid catalyst such as AlCl₃. The reaction of benzene with Cl₂ in presence of AlCl₃ gives chlorobenzene via electrophilic substitution. Therefore, the correct answer is option (b).
```
