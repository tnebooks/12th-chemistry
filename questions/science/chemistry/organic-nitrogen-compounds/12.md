**Nitrobenzene on reaction with conc. HNO$_3$ / H$_2$SO$_4$ at 80-100°C forms which one of the following products?**

```markdown
Nitrobenzene on nitration with conc. HNO₃/H₂SO₄ at 80-100°C gives meta-dinitrobenzene (1,3-dinitrobenzene). The nitro group is meta-directing, so the second nitro group enters at the meta position. Therefore, the correct answer is 1,3-dinitrobenzene.
```
