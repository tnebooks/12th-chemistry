**When aniline reacts with acetic anhydride the product formed is**

```markdown
Aniline reacts with acetic anhydride to form acetanilide (N-phenylacetamide). The reaction is: C₆H₅NH₂ + (CH₃CO)₂O → C₆H₅NHCOCH₃ + CH₃COOH. Therefore, the correct answer is Acetanilide.
```
