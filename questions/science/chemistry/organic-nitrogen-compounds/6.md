**Which one of the following nitro compounds does not react with nitrous acid?**

```markdown
Tertiary nitroalkanes, such as (CH₃)₃C-NO₂, do not react with nitrous acid because they have no hydrogen atom on the carbon bearing the nitro group. Primary and secondary nitroalkanes react with nitrous acid. Therefore, the correct answer is (CH₃)₃C-NO₂.
```
