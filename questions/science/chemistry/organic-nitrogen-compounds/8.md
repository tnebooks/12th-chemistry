**The product formed by the reaction of an aldehyde with a primary amine is**

```markdown
Aldehydes react with primary amines to form Schiff's bases (imines). The general reaction is R-CHO + R'-NH₂ → R-CH=N-R' + H₂O. Therefore, the correct answer is Schiff's base.
```
