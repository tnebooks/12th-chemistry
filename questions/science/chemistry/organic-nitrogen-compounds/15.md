**Which of the following amines does not undergo acetylation?**

```markdown
Triethylamine is a tertiary amine and does not have a hydrogen atom attached to nitrogen. Acetylation requires a replaceable hydrogen on nitrogen. Therefore, triethylamine does not undergo acetylation. t-Butylamine, ethylamine (primary), and diethylamine (secondary) all have N-H bonds and undergo acetylation.
```
