answers:

**CH$_3$CH$_2$Br $\xrightarrow{\text{AgOH}}$ A $\xrightarrow{\text{KMnO}_4/\text{H}^+}$ B $\xrightarrow{\text{NH}_3}$ C $\xrightarrow{\text{Br}_2/\text{NaOH}}$ D**

```markdown
CH₃CH₂Br reacts with AgOH to give ethanol (A). Ethanol on oxidation with KMnO₄/H⁺ gives acetic acid (B). Acetic acid reacts with NH₃ to give acetamide (C). Acetamide on Hofmann bromamide degradation gives methanamine (D). Therefore, the correct answer is methanamine.
```
