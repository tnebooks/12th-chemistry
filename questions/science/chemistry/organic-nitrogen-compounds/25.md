answers:
  - COOH $\xrightarrow{\text{heat}}$ CONH$_2$ (with NH$_2$)

**The major product of the following reaction:**
**COOH + NH$_2$ $\xrightarrow{\text{heat}}$**

```markdown
When a carboxylic acid is heated with an amine, it forms an amide. The carboxylic acid group (-COOH) reacts with the amine group (-NH₂) to form an amide (-CONH₂) with elimination of water. Therefore, the correct product is the amide formed from the carboxylic acid and amine. The correct answer is option (b).
```
