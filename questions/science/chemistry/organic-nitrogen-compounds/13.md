**C$_5$H$_{13}$N reacts with HNO$_2$ to give an optically active compound. The compound is**

```markdown
Pentan-2-amine (CH₃-CH(NH₂)-CH₂-CH₂-CH₃) reacts with HNO₂ to give pentan-2-ol, which is optically active (has a chiral center at C-2). Pentan-1-amine gives pentan-1-ol (not optically active), and tertiary amines do not give alcohols with HNO₂. Therefore, the correct answer is pentan-2-amine.
```
