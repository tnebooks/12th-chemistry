**Aniline + benzoyl chloride $\xrightarrow{\text{NaOH}}$ C$_6$H$_5$-NH-COC$_6$H$_5$. This reaction is known as**

```markdown
The reaction of aniline with benzoyl chloride in the presence of NaOH to form benzanilide (C₆H₅-NH-COC₆H₅) is known as the Schotten-Baumann reaction. It is acylation of amines using acid chlorides in alkaline medium. Therefore, the correct answer is Schotten-Baumann reaction.
```
