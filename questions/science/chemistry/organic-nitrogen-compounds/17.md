**When CH$_3$CH$_2$NO$_2$ is reduced with Sn / HCl the pair of compounds formed are**

```markdown
Nitroethane (CH₃CH₂NO₂) on reduction with Sn/HCl gives ethanol (CH₃CH₂OH) and hydroxylamine hydrochloride (NH₂OH·HCl). Therefore, the correct answer is Ethanol and hydroxylamine hydrochloride.
```
