**Which of the following reagent can be used to convert nitrobenzene to aniline ?**

```markdown
Nitrobenzene can be reduced to aniline using Sn/HCl, Zn/NH₄Cl, or catalytic hydrogenation. However, ZnHg/NaOH is used for Clemmensen reduction (carbonyl to alkane). Among the given options, Sn/HCl is the most commonly used reagent for this reduction. Therefore, the correct answer is Sn/HCl.
```
