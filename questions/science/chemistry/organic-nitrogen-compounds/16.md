**Which one of the following is most basic?**

```markdown
Electron-donating groups increase basicity, while electron-withdrawing groups decrease basicity. Methyl group (CH₃) is electron-donating, while Cl, Br, and NO₂ are electron-withdrawing. Therefore, 2,4-dimethylaniline (with two electron-donating CH₃ groups) is the most basic among the given options.
```
