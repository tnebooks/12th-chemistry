**C$_6$H$_5$NO$_2$ $\xrightarrow{}$ A $\xrightarrow{\text{NaNO}_2/\text{HCl}}$ B $\xrightarrow{\text{H}_2\text{O}}$ C. 'C' is**

```markdown
C₆H₅NO₂ (nitrobenzene) is reduced to aniline (A). Aniline on diazotization with NaNO₂/HCl gives benzene diazonium chloride (B). Benzene diazonium chloride on treatment with water gives phenol (C). Therefore, the correct answer is C₆H₅-OH.
```
