**Which of the following is an analgesic?**

```markdown
Analgesics are pain-relieving drugs. Aspirin is a well-known analgesic (also antipyretic). Streptomycin, Chloromycetin (Chloramphenicol), and Penicillin are antibiotics used against bacterial infections. Therefore, Aspirin is the correct answer.
```
