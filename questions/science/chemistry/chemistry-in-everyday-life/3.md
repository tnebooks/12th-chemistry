**Drugs that bind to the receptor site and inhibit its natural function are called**

```markdown
Drugs that bind to the receptor site and block its natural function are called antagonists. Agonists activate the receptor and mimic the natural function. Enzymes are biological catalysts, and molecular targets are the biomolecules with which drugs interact. Therefore, the correct answer is antagonists.
```
