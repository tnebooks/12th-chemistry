**Antiseptics and disinfectants either kill or prevent growth of microorganisms. Identify which of the following statement is not true.**

```markdown
Dilute solutions of boric acid and hydrogen peroxide are mild antiseptics, not strong antiseptics. Strong antiseptics include substances like iodine and chlorine. Disinfectants harm living tissues, phenol acts as antiseptic at 0.2% and disinfectant at 1%, and chlorine/iodine are strong disinfectants - all these are true statements. Hence the incorrect statement is option (a).
```
