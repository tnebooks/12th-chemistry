**Which one of the following is a bio-degradable polymer?**

```markdown
PHBV (Polyhydroxybutyrate-co-hydroxyvalerate) is a biodegradable polymer used in packaging and medical applications. HDPE, PVC, and Nylon 6 are non-biodegradable synthetic polymers. Therefore, PHBV is the correct answer as it can be degraded by microorganisms.
```
