**Which of the following is a co-polymer?**

```markdown
PHBV is a copolymer made from two monomers: 3-hydroxybutanoic acid and 3-hydroxypentanoic acid. Orlon is a homopolymer of acrylonitrile, PVC is a homopolymer of vinyl chloride, and Teflon is a homopolymer of tetrafluoroethylene. Therefore, PHBV is the correct answer as it is a copolymer.
```
