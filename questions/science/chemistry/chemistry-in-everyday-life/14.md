**Regarding cross-linked or network polymers, which of the following statement is incorrect? (NEET)**

```markdown
Cross-linked polymers contain covalent bonds between various linear polymer chains (cross-links). They are formed from bi and tri-functional monomers, and examples include Bakelite and melamine. However, saying they "contain strong covalent bonds in their polymer chain" is incorrect because this is a characteristic of all polymers, not specifically cross-linked polymers. The defining feature of cross-linked polymers is the cross-links between chains, not just the bonds within the chain.
```
