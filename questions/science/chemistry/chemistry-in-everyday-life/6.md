**Nylon is an example of**

```markdown
Nylon is a polyamide polymer formed by condensation polymerization between a dicarboxylic acid and a diamine. For example, nylon 6,6 is formed from adipic acid and hexamethylenediamine. Therefore, nylon is classified as a polyamide.
```
