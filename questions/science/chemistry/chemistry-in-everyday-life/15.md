**Which one of the following structures represents nylon 6,6 polymer?**

```markdown
Nylon 6,6 is formed by condensation polymerization of adipic acid (hexanedioic acid) and hexamethylenediamine. It has repeating units with the structure -[NH-(CH₂)₆-NH-CO-(CH₂)₄-CO]-. Option (d) correctly represents this polymer structure with alternating amide linkages and hydrocarbon chains of six and four carbon atoms respectively. Therefore, the correct answer is option (d).
```
