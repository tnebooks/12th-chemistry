**Aspirin is a/an**

```markdown
Aspirin is chemically known as acetylsalicylic acid. It is synthesized by acetylation of salicylic acid with acetic anhydride. The other options - benzoyl salicylic acid, chlorobenzoic acid, and anthranilic acid - are different compounds. Hence the correct answer is acetylsalicylic acid.
```
