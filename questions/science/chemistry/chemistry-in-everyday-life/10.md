```markdown
Non-stick cookwares are coated with Teflon (polytetrafluoroethylene or PTFE). The monomer of Teflon is tetrafluoroethylene (1,1,2,2-tetrafluoroethene). Therefore, the correct answer is 1,1,2,2-tetrafluoroethene.
```
