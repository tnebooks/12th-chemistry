**The polymer used in making blankets (artificial wool) is**

```markdown
PAN (Polyacrylonitrile) is used to make artificial wool, also known as acrylic wool or Orlon. It is lightweight, warm, and resembles natural wool. Polystyrene is used in foam products, polyester in fabrics, and polythene in packaging. Therefore, PAN is the correct answer.
```
