**What is the activation energy for a reaction if its rate doubles when the temperature is raised from 200K to 400K? (R = 8.314 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹)**

```markdown
Using Arrhenius equation: ln(k₂/k₁) = Ea/R (1/T₁ - 1/T₂).
Given k₂/k₁ = 2, T₁ = 200K, T₂ = 400K.
ln 2 = Ea/8.314 × (1/200 - 1/400) = Ea/8.314 × (2/400 - 1/400) = Ea/8.314 × 1/400.
0.693 = Ea/(8.314 × 400) → Ea = 0.693 × 8.314 × 400 = 2304.7 J = 2.305 kJ mol⁻¹.
Correct answer is (c) 2.305 kJ mol⁻¹.
```
