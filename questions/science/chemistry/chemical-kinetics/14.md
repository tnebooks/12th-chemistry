**In a first order reaction x → y; if k is the rate constant and the initial concentration of the reactant x is 0.1M, then, the half life is**

```markdown
For a first order reaction, half life is independent of initial concentration: t₁/₂ = ln 2 / k = 0.693/k. It does not depend on [A]₀. Both (a) log 2/k and (c) ln 2/k are correct since log is base 10 and ln is natural log. The correct expression is (c) ln 2/k. Answer is (c).
```
