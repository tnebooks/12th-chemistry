**Among the following graphs showing variation of rate constant with temperature (T) for a reaction, the one that exhibits Arrhenius behavior over the entire temperature range is**

```markdown
According to Arrhenius equation: k = A e^(-Ea/RT). Taking natural log: ln k = ln A - Ea/RT. A plot of ln k vs 1/T gives a straight line with slope -Ea/R. The graph showing this relationship correctly is (b). The graph that shows k increasing exponentially with T also represents Arrhenius behavior. Correct answer is (b).
```
