**A zero order reaction X → Product, with an initial concentration 0.02M has a half life of 10 min. If one starts with concentration 0.04M, then the half life is**

```markdown
For a zero order reaction: t₁/₂ = [A]₀ / 2k. Half life is directly proportional to initial concentration. If [A]₀ is doubled from 0.02M to 0.04M, the half life also doubles. Therefore t₁/₂ = 2 × 10 = 20 minutes. Correct answer is (c) 20 min.
```
