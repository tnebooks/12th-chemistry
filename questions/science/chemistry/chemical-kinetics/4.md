**For a first order reaction A → product with initial concentration x mol L⁻¹, has a half life period of 2.5 hours. For the same reaction with initial concentration (x/2) mol L⁻¹ the half life is**

```markdown
For a first order reaction, half life is independent of initial concentration: t₁/₂ = ln 2 / k. It does not depend on [A]₀. Therefore, even if the initial concentration changes from x to x/2, the half life remains the same: 2.5 hours. Correct answer is (c) 2.5 hours.
```
