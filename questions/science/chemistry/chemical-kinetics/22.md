**If 75% of a first order reaction was completed in 60 minutes, 50% of the same reaction under the same conditions would be completed in**

```markdown
For first order: t = (2.303/k) log([A]₀/[A]).
For 75% completion: [A] = 25% of [A]₀, so [A]₀/[A] = 4.
60 = (2.303/k) log 4 = (2.303/k) × 0.602 = (1.386/k). So k = 1.386/60 = 0.0231 min⁻¹.
For 50% completion: t₁/₂ = 0.693/k = 0.693/0.0231 = 30 minutes.
Correct answer is (b) 30 minutes.
```
