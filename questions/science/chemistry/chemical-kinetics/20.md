**If the initial concentration of the reactant is doubled, the time for half reaction is also doubled. Then the order of the reaction is**

```markdown
For zero order reaction: t₁/₂ ∝ [A]₀. If [A]₀ is doubled, t₁/₂ also doubles. For first order: t₁/₂ is independent of [A]₀. For second order: t₁/₂ ∝ 1/[A]₀, so doubling [A]₀ halves t₁/₂. Therefore, the order is zero. Correct answer is (a) Zero.
```
