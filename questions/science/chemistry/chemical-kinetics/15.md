**Predict the rate law of the following reaction based on the data given below**
2A + B → C + 3D

| Reaction number | [A] (min) | [B] (min) | Initial rate (M s⁻¹) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.1 | 0.1 | x |
| 2 | 0.2 | 0.1 | 2x |
| 3 | 0.1 | 0.2 | 4x |
| 4 | 0.2 | 0.2 | 8x |

```markdown
Compare 1 and 2: [A] doubles, [B] constant, rate doubles → order w.r.t A = 1.
Compare 1 and 3: [A] constant, [B] doubles, rate becomes 4x (quadruples) → order w.r.t B = 2.
Compare 1 and 4: both double, rate becomes 8x → 2¹ × 2² = 2³ = 8.
Rate law: rate = k[A][B]². Correct answer is (b).
```
