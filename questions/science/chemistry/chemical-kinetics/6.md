**The decomposition of phosphine (PH₃) on tungsten at low pressure is a first order reaction. It is because the (NEET)**

```markdown
At low pressure, the surface coverage is proportional to the pressure (or concentration) of the gas. The rate of reaction is proportional to the surface coverage. Therefore, rate ∝ [PH₃], which makes it a first order reaction. This is consistent with the Langmuir adsorption isotherm at low pressure. Correct answer is (a) rate is proportional to the surface coverage.
```
