**The rate constant of a reaction is 5.8 × 10⁻² s⁻¹. The order of the reaction is**

```markdown
For a first order reaction, the units of rate constant are s⁻¹ or time⁻¹. Here k = 5.8 × 10⁻² s⁻¹ has units of s⁻¹, which indicates a first order reaction. For zero order, units are mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹. For second order, units are L mol⁻¹ s⁻¹. Correct answer is (a) First order.
```
