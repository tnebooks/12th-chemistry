answers:
  - k = (2.303/t) log(2P₀/(3P₀ - P))
---

**In A → B + C + D, the initial pressure was P₀, and after time t it was P. Expressing the rate constant in terms of P₀, P and t, we will be**

```markdown
For the reaction A → B + C + D, if initial pressure is P₀, let x be the pressure of A reacted.
Total pressure at time t: P = (P₀ - x) + x + x + x = P₀ + 2x.
So x = (P - P₀)/2.
Partial pressure of A at time t = P₀ - x = P₀ - (P - P₀)/2 = (3P₀ - P)/2.
For first order: k = (2.303/t) log(P₀/(P₀ - x)) = (2.303/t) log(P₀ / ((3P₀ - P)/2)).
k = (2.303/t) log(2P₀/(3P₀ - P)).
Correct answer is (a).
```
