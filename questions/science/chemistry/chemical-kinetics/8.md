**The addition of a catalyst during a chemical reaction alters which of the following quantities? (NEET)**

```markdown
A catalyst provides an alternative pathway for the reaction with lower activation energy. It does not change the enthalpy (ΔH), entropy (ΔS), or internal energy (ΔU) of the reaction. It only lowers the activation energy (Ea) by providing a different mechanism. Correct answer is (b) Activation energy.
```
