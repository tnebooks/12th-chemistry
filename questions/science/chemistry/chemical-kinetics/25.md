**After 2 hours, a radioactive substance becomes (1/16) of original amount. Then the half life (in min) is**

```markdown
Amount remaining = (1/2)ⁿ = 1/16 = (1/2)⁴. So n = 4 half lives.
2 hours = 120 minutes. 4 half lives = 120 minutes.
1 half life = 120/4 = 30 minutes.
Correct answer is (c) 30 minutes.
```
