**For the reaction N₂O₅(g) → 2NO₂(g) + 1/2O₂(g), the value of rate of disappearance of N₂O₅ is given as 6.5 × 10⁻² mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹. The rate of formation of NO₂ and O₂ is given respectively as**

```markdown
Rate = -d[N₂O₅]/dt = 1/2 d[NO₂]/dt = 2 d[O₂]/dt.
Given -d[N₂O₅]/dt = 6.5 × 10⁻² mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹.
d[NO₂]/dt = 2 × 6.5 × 10⁻² = 1.3 × 10⁻¹ mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹.
d[O₂]/dt = 1/2 × 6.5 × 10⁻² = 3.25 × 10⁻² mol L⁻¹ s⁻¹.
Correct answer is (a).
```
