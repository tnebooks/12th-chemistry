**During the decomposition of H₂O₂, to give dihydrogen, 48 g O₂ is formed per minute at certain point of time. The rate of formation of water at this point is**

```markdown
Decomposition of H₂O₂: 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂.
Molar mass of O₂ = 32 g/mol. 48 g O₂ = 48/32 = 1.5 mol O₂ per minute.
From stoichiometry: 1 mol O₂ is formed with 2 mol H₂O.
So, rate of formation of H₂O = 2 × 1.5 = 3.0 mol min⁻¹.
Correct answer is (d) 3.0 mol min⁻¹.
```
