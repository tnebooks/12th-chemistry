**The number of electrons that have a total charge of 9650 coulombs is**

```markdown
1 Faraday = 96500 C = 1 mole of electrons = 6.022 × 10²³ electrons
9650 C = (9650/96500) × 6.022 × 10²³ = 0.1 × 6.022 × 10²³ = 6.022 × 10²² electrons
Correct answer is (c) 6.022 × 10²².
```
