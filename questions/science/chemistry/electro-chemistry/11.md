**Which of the following electrolytic solution has the least specific conductance?**

```markdown
Specific conductance (κ) decreases with dilution. Among the given options, 0.002N is the most dilute solution. Therefore, it has the least specific conductance.
Correct answer is (b) 0.002N.
```
