---
choices:
  - "PbSO₄ on anode is oxidized to PbO₂"
  - "PbSO₄ on anode is reduced to Pb"
  - "PbSO₄ on cathode is oxidized to PbO₂"
answers:
  - "PbSO₄ on cathode is reduced to Pb"
---

**While charging lead storage battery**

```markdown
During charging of lead storage battery:
At cathode (reduction): PbSO₄(s) + 2e⁻ → Pb(s) + SO₄²⁻(aq)
At anode (oxidation): PbSO₄(s) + 2H₂O(l) → PbO₂(s) + SO₄²⁻(aq) + 4H⁺ + 2e⁻
So PbSO₄ on cathode is reduced to Pb.
Correct answer is (a).
```
