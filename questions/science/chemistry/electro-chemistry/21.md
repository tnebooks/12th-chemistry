**Consider the change in oxidation state of Bromine corresponding to different emf values: BrO₄⁻ →¹·⁸²ᵛ BrO₃⁻ →¹·⁵ᵛ HBrO →¹·⁵⁹⁵ᵛ Br₂ →¹·⁰⁶⁵²ᵛ Br⁻. Then the species undergoing disproportionation is**

```markdown
Disproportionation occurs when a species is simultaneously oxidized and reduced.
For HBrO: It can be oxidized to BrO₃⁻ (higher oxidation state) or reduced to Br₂ (lower oxidation state).
E°cell for HBrO → BrO₃⁻ + Br₂:
E°cell = E°ox + E°red = (-1.5) + 1.595 = +0.095 V (positive, spontaneous)
So HBrO undergoes disproportionation.
Correct answer is (d) HBrO.
```
