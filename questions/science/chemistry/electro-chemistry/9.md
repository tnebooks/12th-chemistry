**During electrolysis of molten sodium chloride, the time required to produce 0.1 mole of chlorine gas using a current of 3A is**

```markdown
2Cl⁻ → Cl₂ + 2e⁻
1 mole of Cl₂ requires 2 moles of electrons = 2F = 2 × 96500 C
0.1 mole of Cl₂ requires 0.1 × 2 × 96500 = 19300 C
Q = I × t → t = Q/I = 19300/3 = 6433.33 sec = 107.2 minutes
Correct answer is (b) 107.2 minutes.
```
