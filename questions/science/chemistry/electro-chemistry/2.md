**Consider the following half cell reactions: Mn²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Mn E° = -1.18 V; Mn²⁺ → Mn³⁺ + e⁻ E° = -1.51 V. The E° for the reaction 3Mn²⁺ → Mn + 2Mn³⁺, and the possibility of the forward reaction are respectively.**

```markdown
For the reaction: 3Mn²⁺ → Mn + 2Mn³⁺
Cathode (reduction): Mn²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Mn, E°red = -1.18 V
Anode (oxidation): 2[Mn²⁺ → Mn³⁺ + e⁻], E°ox = -1.51 V
E°cell = E°ox + E°red = -1.51 + (-1.18) = -2.69 V
Since E°cell is negative, ΔG° is positive, so the forward reaction is non-spontaneous.
Correct answer is (b) -2.69V and non spontaneous.
```
