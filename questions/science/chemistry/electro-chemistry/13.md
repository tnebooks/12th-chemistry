**Among the following cells: I) Leclanche cell, II) Nickel-Cadmium cell, III) Lead storage battery, IV) Mercury cell. Primary cells are**

```markdown
Primary cells are non-rechargeable cells where the reaction occurs only once.
Leclanche cell (dry cell) and Mercury cell are primary cells.
Nickel-Cadmium cell and Lead storage battery are secondary cells (rechargeable).
Correct answer is (a) I and IV.
```
