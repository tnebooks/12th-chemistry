**For the cell reaction 2Fe³⁺(aq) + 2I⁻(aq) → 2Fe²⁺(aq) + I₂(aq), E°cell = 0.24 V at 298K. The standard Gibbs energy (ΔG°) of the cell reaction is:**

```markdown
ΔG° = -nFE°cell
n = 2 (number of electrons transferred)
F = 96500 C/mol
E°cell = 0.24 V
ΔG° = -2 × 96500 × 0.24 = -46320 J/mol = -46.32 KJ mol⁻¹
Correct answer is (a) -46.32 KJ mol⁻¹.
```
