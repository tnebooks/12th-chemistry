**A conductivity cell has been calibrated with a 0.01M, 1:1 electrolytic solution (specific conductance κ = 1.25 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹) and the measured resistance was 800 Ω at 25°C. The cell constant is**

```markdown
Cell constant (G*) = κ × R
G* = 1.25 × 10⁻³ × 800 = 1.0 cm⁻¹
Correct answer is (c) 1 cm⁻¹.
```
