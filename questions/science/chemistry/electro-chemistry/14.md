**Zinc can be coated on iron to produce galvanized iron but the reverse is not possible. It is because**

```markdown
Zinc has higher negative electrode potential (E°Zn²⁺/Zn = -0.76V) than iron (E°Fe²⁺/Fe = -0.44V). Zinc is more reactive and acts as a sacrificial anode, protecting iron from corrosion. Iron cannot be coated on zinc because zinc would corrode first.
Correct answer is (d) Zinc has higher negative electrode potential than iron.
```
