---
choices:
  - "E₁ < E₂"
  - "E₂ ≥ E₁"
  - "E₁ = E₂"
answers:
  - "E₁ > E₂"
---

**In the electrochemical cell: Zn | ZnSO₄ (0.01 M) || CuSO₄ (1.0 M) | Cu, the emf of this Daniel cell is E₁. When the concentration of ZnSO₄ is changed to 1.0M and CuSO₄ changed to 0.01M, the emf changes to E₂. Which one is the relationship between E₁ and E₂?**

```markdown
Nernst equation for Daniel cell:
E = E° - (0.0591/2) log([Zn²⁺]/[Cu²⁺])
E₁ = E° - (0.0591/2) log(0.01/1) = E° - (0.0591/2) log(10⁻²) = E° + 0.0591
E₂ = E° - (0.0591/2) log(1/0.01) = E° - (0.0591/2) log(10²) = E° - 0.0591
Therefore E₁ > E₂.
Correct answer is (b) E₁ > E₂.
```
