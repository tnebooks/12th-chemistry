**The molar conductivity of a 0.5 mol dm⁻³ solution of AgNO₃ with electrolytic conductivity of 5.76 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹ at 298 K is**

```markdown
Molar conductivity: Λm = κ × 1000 / C
κ = 5.76 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹
C = 0.5 mol dm⁻³ = 0.5 mol L⁻¹
Λm = (5.76 × 10⁻³ × 1000) / 0.5 = 5.76 / 0.5 = 11.52 S cm² mol⁻¹
Correct answer is (b) 11.52 S cm² mol⁻¹.
```
