**Assertion: pure iron when heated in dry air is converted with a layer of rust. Reason: Rust has the composition Fe₃O₄**

```markdown
Assertion is false - pure iron when heated in dry air does not rust; rust requires moisture and oxygen. Reason is also false - rust has the composition Fe₂O₃·xH₂O (hydrated ferric oxide), not Fe₃O₄. Both are false.
Correct answer is (d) both assertion and reason are false.
```
