**A gas X at 1 atm is bubbled through a solution containing a mixture of 1M Y⁻ and 1M Z⁻ at 25°C. If the reduction potential of Z⁻ > Y⁻ > X₂, then**

```markdown
Higher reduction potential means stronger oxidizing agent.
If Z⁻ > Y⁻ > X₂, then X₂ has the lowest reduction potential.
A stronger oxidizing agent (Y) can oxidize a weaker oxidizing agent's conjugate base.
Y will oxidize both X and Z since Y has higher reduction potential than both.
Correct answer is (c) Y will oxidize both X and Z.
```
