**How many faradays of electricity are required for the following reaction to occur: MnO₄⁻ → Mn²⁺**

```markdown
MnO₄⁻ → Mn²⁺
In MnO₄⁻, Mn is in +7 oxidation state. In Mn²⁺, Mn is in +2 oxidation state.
Change in oxidation state = 7 - 2 = 5.
So 5 moles of electrons (5F) are required.
Correct answer is (a) 5F.
```
