**The number of electrons delivered at the cathode during electrolysis by a current of 1A in 60 seconds is (charge of electron = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C)**

```markdown
Q = I × t = 1 × 60 = 60 C
Number of electrons = Q / charge of 1 electron = 60 / (1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹) = 3.75 × 10²⁰ electrons
Correct answer is (c) 3.75 × 10²⁰.
```
