**Conductivity of a saturated solution of a sparingly soluble salt AB (1:1 electrolyte) at 298K is 1.85 × 10⁻⁵ S m⁻¹. Solubility product of the salt AB at 298K (Λ°m)AB = 14 × 10⁻³ S m² mol⁻¹**

```markdown
For sparingly soluble salt, conductivity of solution ≈ conductivity of salt (since water conductivity is negligible)
κ = 1.85 × 10⁻⁵ S m⁻¹
Λ°m = 14 × 10⁻³ S m² mol⁻¹
Solubility (s) = κ / Λ°m = 1.85 × 10⁻⁵ / 14 × 10⁻³ = 1.321 × 10⁻³ mol m⁻³ = 1.321 × 10⁻⁶ mol L⁻¹
For AB type salt: Ksp = s² = (1.321 × 10⁻⁶)² = 1.74 × 10⁻¹²
Correct answer is (d) 1.74 × 10⁻¹².
```
