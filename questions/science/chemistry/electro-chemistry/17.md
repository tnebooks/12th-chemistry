**The equivalent conductance of M/36 solution of a weak monobasic acid is 6 mho cm² equivalent⁻¹ and at infinite dilution is 400 mho cm² equivalent⁻¹. The dissociation constant of this acid is**

```markdown
Degree of dissociation: α = Λ/Λ° = 6/400 = 0.015
C = M/36 = 1/36 M
Ka = C × α² / (1-α) ≈ C × α² (since α is small)
Ka = (1/36) × (0.015)² = (1/36) × 2.25 × 10⁻⁴ = 6.25 × 10⁻⁶
Correct answer is (b) 6.25 × 10⁻⁶.
```
