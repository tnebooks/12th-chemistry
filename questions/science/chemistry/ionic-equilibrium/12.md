**The solubility of AgCl (s) with solubility product 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰ in 0.1M NaCl solution would be**

```markdown
AgCl(s) ⇌ Ag⁺ + Cl⁻
Ksp = [Ag⁺][Cl⁻] = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰
In 0.1 M NaCl, [Cl⁻] = 0.1 M (from NaCl) + s (from AgCl) ≈ 0.1 M (since s is very small)
[Ag⁺] = Ksp/[Cl⁻] = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁰ / 0.1 = 1.6 × 10⁻⁹ M
Correct answer is (b) 1.6 × 10⁻⁹ M.
```
