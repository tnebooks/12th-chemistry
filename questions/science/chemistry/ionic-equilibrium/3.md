**The solubility of BaSO₄ in water is 2.42 × 10⁻³ g L⁻¹ at 298K. The value of its solubility product (Ksp) will be (NEET - 2018). (Given molar mass of BaSO₄ = 233 g mol⁻¹)**

```markdown
BaSO₄ ⇌ Ba²⁺ + SO₄²⁻
Solubility in mol L⁻¹ = 2.42 × 10⁻³ / 233 = 1.038 × 10⁻⁵ M
Ksp = s² = (1.038 × 10⁻⁵)² = 1.08 × 10⁻¹⁰ mol² L⁻²
Correct answer is (c) 1.08 × 10⁻¹⁰ mol² L⁻².
```
