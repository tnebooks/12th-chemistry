**Dissociation constant of NH₄OH is 1.8 × 10⁻⁵. The hydrolysis constant of NH₄Cl would be**

```markdown
NH₄Cl is a salt of weak base (NH₄OH) and strong acid (HCl).
For salt of weak base and strong acid:
Kh = Kw / Kb = 1 × 10⁻¹⁴ / 1.8 × 10⁻⁵ = 5.55 × 10⁻¹⁰
Correct answer is (b) 5.55 × 10⁻¹⁰.
```
