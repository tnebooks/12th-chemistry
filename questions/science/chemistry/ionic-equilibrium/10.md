**The percentage of pyridine (C₅H₅N) that forms pyridinium ion (C₅H₅NH⁺) in a 0.10M aqueous pyridine solution (Kb for C₅H₅N = 1.7 × 10⁻⁹) is**

```markdown
C₅H₅N + H₂O ⇌ C₅H₅NH⁺ + OH⁻
Kb = α²C / (1-α) ≈ α²C (since α is small)
α = √(Kb/C) = √(1.7 × 10⁻⁹ / 0.1) = √(1.7 × 10⁻⁸) = 1.3 × 10⁻⁴
Percentage = 1.3 × 10⁻⁴ × 100 = 0.013%
Correct answer is (b) 0.013%.
```
