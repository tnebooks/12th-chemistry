**Which of the following fluoro compounds is most likely to behave as a Lewis base? (NEET – 2016)**

```markdown
BF₃ - electron deficient (Lewis acid).
PF₃ - has a lone pair of electrons on P, can donate electrons (Lewis base).
CF₄ - no lone pair, neutral.
SiF₄ - no lone pair, neutral.
Correct answer is (b) PF₃.
```
