**Concentration of the Ag⁺ ions in a saturated solution of Ag₂C₂O₄ is 2.24 × 10⁻⁴ mol L⁻¹. Solubility product of Ag₂C₂O₄ is (NEET – 2017)**

```markdown
Ag₂C₂O₄ ⇌ 2Ag⁺ + C₂O₄²⁻
[Ag⁺] = 2.24 × 10⁻⁴ M
[C₂O₄²⁻] = [Ag⁺]/2 = 1.12 × 10⁻⁴ M
Ksp = [Ag⁺]²[C₂O₄²⁻] = (2.24 × 10⁻⁴)² × (1.12 × 10⁻⁴) = 5.619 × 10⁻¹² mol³ L⁻³
Correct answer is (d) 5.619 × 10⁻¹² mol³ L⁻³.
```
