---
choices:
  - "[H⁺] = Ka[salt]"
  - "[H⁺] = Ka[acid]"
  - "[H⁺] = Ka[salt]/[acid]"
answers:
  - "[H⁺] = Ka[acid]/[salt]"
---

**The hydrogen ion concentration of a buffer solution consisting of a weak acid and its salts is given by**

```markdown
Henderson-Hasselbalch equation:
pH = pKa + log([Salt]/[Acid])
But [H⁺] = Ka × [Acid]/[Salt]
This is derived from Ka = [H⁺][Salt]/[Acid]
Correct answer is (a) [H⁺] = Ka[acid]/[salt].
```
