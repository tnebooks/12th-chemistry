**Which of the following relation is correct for degree of hydrolysis of ammonium acetate?**

```markdown
Ammonium acetate (NH₄OAc) is a salt of weak acid (CH₃COOH) and weak base (NH₄OH).
For salt of weak acid and weak base:
h = √(Kw / (Ka × Kb))
Correct answer is (c) h = √(Kw/(Ka·Kb)).
```
