**Following solutions were prepared by mixing different volumes of NaOH and HCl of different concentrations. (NEET – 2018)**
i. 60 mL HCl + 40 mL NaOH
ii. 55 mL HCl + 45 mL NaOH
iii. 75 mL HCl + 25 mL NaOH
iv. 100 mL HCl + 100 mL NaOH
**pH of which one of them will be equal to 1?**

```markdown
For (iii): 75 mL of 0.2 M HCl + 25 mL of 0.2 M NaOH
Moles of HCl = 0.2 × 75 × 10⁻³ = 15 × 10⁻³
Moles of NaOH = 0.2 × 25 × 10⁻³ = 5 × 10⁻³
Moles of HCl remaining = 10 × 10⁻³
Total volume = 100 mL = 100 × 10⁻³ L
[HCl] = 10 × 10⁻³ / 100 × 10⁻³ = 0.1 M
pH = -log(0.1) = 1
Correct answer is (d) iii.
```
