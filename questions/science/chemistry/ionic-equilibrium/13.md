---

**If the solubility product of lead iodide is 3.2 × 10⁻⁸, its solubility will be**

```markdown
PbI₂ ⇌ Pb²⁺ + 2I⁻
Ksp = s(2s)² = 4s³
3.2 × 10⁻⁸ = 4s³
s³ = 8 × 10⁻⁹
s = (8 × 10⁻⁹)^(1/3) = 2 × 10⁻³ M
Correct answer is (a) 2 × 10⁻³ M.
```
