**Equal volumes of three acid solutions of pH 1, 2 and 3 are mixed in a vessel. What will be the H⁺ ion concentration in the mixture?**

```markdown
pH = -log[H⁺]; [H⁺] = 10⁻pH
For pH 1: [H⁺] = 10⁻¹ = 0.1 M
For pH 2: [H⁺] = 10⁻² = 0.01 M
For pH 3: [H⁺] = 10⁻³ = 0.001 M
Let volume of each be x mL.
Total [H⁺] = x(0.1 + 0.01 + 0.001) / 3x = (0.111)/3 = 0.037 = 3.7 × 10⁻² M
Correct answer is (a) 3.7 × 10⁻².
```
