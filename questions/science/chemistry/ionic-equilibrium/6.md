**Which will make basic buffer?**

```markdown
A basic buffer is made from a weak base and its salt with a strong acid.
HCl + NH₄OH → NH₄Cl + H₂O
Here, 100 mL of 0.1 M HCl reacts with 100 mL of 0.1 M NH₄OH to form NH₄Cl, and the remaining 100 mL of 0.1 M NH₄OH (weak base) is left. This gives a mixture of weak base (NH₄OH) and its salt (NH₄Cl) - a basic buffer.
Correct answer is (c).
```
