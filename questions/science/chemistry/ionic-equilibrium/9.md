**The aqueous solutions of sodium formate, anilinium chloride and potassium cyanide are respectively**

```markdown
Sodium formate (HCOONa) - salt of weak acid (HCOOH) and strong base (NaOH) → basic.
Anilinium chloride (C₆H₅NH₃Cl) - salt of weak base (C₆H₅NH₂) and strong acid (HCl) → acidic.
Potassium cyanide (KCN) - salt of weak acid (HCN) and strong base (KOH) → basic.
Correct answer is (b) basic, acidic, basic.
```
