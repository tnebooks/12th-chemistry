**MY and NY₃, are insoluble salts and have the same Ksp values of 6.2 × 10⁻¹³ at room temperature. Which statement would be true with regard to MY and NY₃?**

```markdown
For MY: Ksp = s² → s = √(6.2 × 10⁻¹³) = 7.87 × 10⁻⁷ M
For NY₃: Ksp = s(3s)³ = 27s⁴ → s = (6.2 × 10⁻¹³ / 27)^(1/4) = 3.9 × 10⁻⁴ M
The molar solubility of MY (7.87 × 10⁻⁷) is less than that of NY₃ (3.9 × 10⁻⁴).
Common ion effect decreases solubility, so (a) and (b) are false.
Correct answer is (d).
```
