**What is the pH of the resulting solution when equal volumes of 0.1M NaOH and 0.01M HCl are mixed?**

```markdown
Let volume be x mL each.
Moles of NaOH = 0.1 × x × 10⁻³ = 0.1x × 10⁻³
Moles of HCl = 0.01 × x × 10⁻³ = 0.01x × 10⁻³
NaOH remaining = 0.09x × 10⁻³ moles
Total volume = 2x × 10⁻³ L
[OH⁻] = 0.09x × 10⁻³ / 2x × 10⁻³ = 0.045 M
pOH = -log(0.045) = -log(4.5 × 10⁻²) = 2 - log 4.5 = 2 - 0.65 = 1.35
pH = 14 - 1.35 = 12.65
Correct answer is (d) 12.65.
```
