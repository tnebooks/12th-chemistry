**pH of a saturated solution of Ca(OH)₂ is 9. The Solubility product (Ksp) of Ca(OH)₂**

```markdown
Ca(OH)₂ ⇌ Ca²⁺ + 2OH⁻
pH = 9, pOH = 14 - 9 = 5
[OH⁻] = 10⁻⁵ M
[Ca²⁺] = [OH⁻]/2 = 0.5 × 10⁻⁵ M
Ksp = [Ca²⁺][OH⁻]² = (0.5 × 10⁻⁵) × (10⁻⁵)² = 0.5 × 10⁻¹⁵
Correct answer is (a) 0.5 × 10⁻¹⁵.
```
