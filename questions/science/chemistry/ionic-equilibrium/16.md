**The dissociation constant of a weak acid is 1 × 10⁻³. In order to prepare a buffer solution with a pH = 4, the [Acid]/[Salt] ratio should be**

```markdown
Henderson-Hasselbalch equation: pH = pKa + log([Salt]/[Acid])
pKa = -log(1 × 10⁻³) = 3
4 = 3 + log([Salt]/[Acid])
log([Salt]/[Acid]) = 1
[Salt]/[Acid] = 10
[Acid]/[Salt] = 1/10 = 1:10
Correct answer is (d) 1:10.
```
