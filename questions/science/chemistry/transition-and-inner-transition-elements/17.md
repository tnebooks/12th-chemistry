**The most common oxidation state of actinoids is**

```markdown
Like lanthanoids, the most common and stable oxidation state of actinoids is +3. However, actinoids show a wider range of oxidation states from +2 to +7 due to greater participation of 5f, 6d, and 7s orbitals. U shows +6, Np shows +7, Pu shows +6, but +3 is the most common oxidation state.
```
