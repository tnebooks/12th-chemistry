**The number of moles of acidified KMnO₄ required to oxidize 1 mole of ferrous oxalate (FeC₂O₄) is**

```markdown
FeC₂O₄ contains Fe²⁺ (loses 1 electron) and C₂O₄²⁻ (loses 2 electrons) - total 3 electrons lost per mole. MnO₄⁻ accepts 5 electrons in acidic medium. FeC₂O₄ (n=3) : KMnO₄ (n=5) ratio. Moles of KMnO₄ required = 3/5 = 0.6 moles. Therefore, 0.6 moles of acidified KMnO₄ is required to oxidize 1 mole of ferrous oxalate.
```
