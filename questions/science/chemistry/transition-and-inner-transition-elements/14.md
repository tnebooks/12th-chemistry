**Which of the following lanthanoid ion is diamagnetic?**

```markdown
Yb²⁺ has electronic configuration [Xe]4f¹⁴ - completely filled f-orbital with no unpaired electrons. Therefore, it is diamagnetic. Eu²⁺ (4f⁷ - 7 unpaired electrons), Sm²⁺ (4f⁶), Ce²⁺ (4f²) all have unpaired electrons and are paramagnetic. Therefore, Yb²⁺ is the diamagnetic ion.
```
