**Which of the following d block element has half filled penultimate d sub shell as well as half filled valence sub shell?**

```markdown
Cr has electronic configuration [Ar]3d⁵4s¹. Here, the penultimate d-subshell (3d) is half-filled with 5 electrons, and the valence subshell (4s) is also half-filled with 1 electron. Pd has [Kr]4d¹⁰ (completely filled), Pt has [Xe]4f¹⁴5d⁹6s¹. Therefore, only Cr satisfies this condition.
```
