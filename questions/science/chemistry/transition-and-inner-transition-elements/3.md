**Among the transition metals of 3d series, the one that has highest negative M²⁺/M standard electrode potential is**

```markdown
Standard electrode potential values: Ti²⁺/Ti = -1.63V, Cu²⁺/Cu = +0.34V, Mn²⁺/Mn = -1.18V, Zn²⁺/Zn = -0.76V. Ti has the most negative value (-1.63V). Higher negative value indicates higher reducing power and greater tendency to get oxidized. Therefore, Ti is the correct answer.
```
