**The actinoid elements which show the highest oxidation state of +7 are**

```markdown
Np (Neptunium), Pu (Plutonium), and Am (Americium) exhibit +7 oxidation state. Np⁷⁺, Pu⁷⁺, and Am⁷⁺ are known compounds. U shows +6 (not +7), Th shows +4, Fm shows +3, Md shows +3, Es shows +3, No shows +2, and Lr shows +3. Therefore, Np, Pu, Am is the correct answer.
```
