**Permanganate ion changes to ______ in acidic medium**

```markdown
In acidic medium (presence of H⁺ ions), MnO₄⁻ (permanganate ion) accepts 5 electrons and gets reduced to Mn²⁺. MnO₄⁻ + 8H⁺ + 5e⁻ → Mn²⁺ + 4H₂O. In neutral or alkaline medium, it changes to MnO₂, and in strong alkaline medium, it changes to MnO₄²⁻. Therefore, in acidic medium, it changes to Mn²⁺.
```
