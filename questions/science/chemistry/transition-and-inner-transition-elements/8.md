**In acid medium, potassium permanganate oxidizes oxalic acid to**

```markdown
In acidic medium, KMnO₄ oxidizes oxalic acid (H₂C₂O₄) to carbon dioxide (CO₂) and water. The reaction is: 5(COO)₂²⁻ + 2MnO₄⁻ + 16H⁺ → 2Mn²⁺ + 10CO₂↑ + 8H₂O. Oxalic acid is oxidized to CO₂, while MnO₄⁻ is reduced to Mn²⁺. Therefore, carbon dioxide is produced.
```
