**Which one of the following ions has the same number of unpaired electrons as present in V³⁺?**

```markdown
V³⁺ has electronic configuration [Ar]3d² - 2 unpaired electrons. Ti³⁺ - [Ar]3d¹ (1 unpaired), Fe³⁺ - [Ar]3d⁵ (5 unpaired), Ni²⁺ - [Ar]3d⁸ (2 unpaired), Cr³⁺ - [Ar]3d³ (3 unpaired). Ni²⁺ has exactly 2 unpaired electrons, same as V³⁺. Therefore, Ni²⁺ is the correct answer.
```
