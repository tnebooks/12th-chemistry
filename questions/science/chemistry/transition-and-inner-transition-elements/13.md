**Which one of the following statements related to lanthanons is incorrect?**

```markdown
Lanthanons are less reactive than aluminium, not more. Their metallic behaviour is similar to that of aluminium. Eu²⁺ is stable (4f⁷ half-filled), basicity decreases from La to Lu due to lanthanide contraction, and Ce⁴⁺ is used as an oxidizing agent in volumetric analysis. Therefore, "All lanthanons are much more reactive than aluminium" is the incorrect statement.
```
