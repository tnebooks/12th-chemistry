**The magnetic moment of Mn²⁺ ion is**

```markdown
Mn²⁺ has electronic configuration [Ar]3d⁵ - 5 unpaired electrons. Magnetic moment formula: μ = √[n(n+2)] BM, where n = number of unpaired electrons. μ = √[5(5+2)] = √35 = 5.92 BM. Therefore, the magnetic moment of Mn²⁺ is 5.92 BM.
```
