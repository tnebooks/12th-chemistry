**Which one of the following is not correct?**

```markdown
La(OH)₃ is more basic than Lu(OH)₃, not less. As ionic radius decreases from La³⁺ to Lu³⁺ (lanthanide contraction), the M-O bond strength increases, making it harder to release OH⁻ ions. Thus basicity decreases from La to Lu. All other statements are correct: Ln³⁺ radius decreases, La is a transition metal (with [Xe]5d¹6s²), and Zr/Hf have similar radii due to lanthanide contraction.
```
