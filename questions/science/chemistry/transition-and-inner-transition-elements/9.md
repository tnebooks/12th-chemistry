**Which of the following statements is not true?**

```markdown
K₂Cr₂O₇ (potassium dichromate) is highly pure, stable, and does not absorb moisture from air. Therefore, it is preferred over Na₂Cr₂O₇ in volumetric analysis. All other statements are true: H₂S gives S (milky white), acidic K₂Cr₂O₇ is orange, and at pH > 7 it becomes yellow (CrO₄²⁻). So, Na₂Cr₂O₇ being preferred over K₂Cr₂O₇ is the false statement.
```
