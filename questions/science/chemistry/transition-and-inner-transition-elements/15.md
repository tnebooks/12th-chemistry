**Which of the following oxidation state is most common among the lanthanoids?**

```markdown
The most common and stable oxidation state among lanthanoids is +3. This is because lanthanoids lose three electrons (two from 6s and one from 4f or 5d) to attain a stable configuration. Eu²⁺ (4f⁷) and Ce⁴⁺ (4f⁰) are special cases with extra stability, but +3 is the most common oxidation state.
```
