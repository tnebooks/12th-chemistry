**The catalytic behaviour of transition metals and their compounds is ascribed mainly due to**

```markdown
Transition metals and their compounds act as catalysts because they can adopt variable oxidation states. This allows them to form intermediate complexes with reactants and facilitate the reaction. The ability to change oxidation states enables them to accept and donate electrons easily. Unfilled d-orbitals also help, but variable oxidation states is the primary reason.
```
