**How many moles of I₂ are liberated when 1 mole of potassium dichromate react with potassium iodide?**

```markdown
The balanced reaction is: K₂Cr₂O₇ + 6KI + 7H₂SO₄ → 4K₂SO₄ + Cr₂(SO₄)₃ + 7H₂O + 3I₂. According to the equation, 1 mole of K₂Cr₂O₇ liberates 3 moles of I₂. Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 14H⁺ + 6e⁻ → 2Cr³⁺ + 7H₂O and 6I⁻ → 3I₂ + 6e⁻. Therefore, 3 moles of iodine are liberated.
```
