**Which of the following is strongest acid among all?**

```markdown
Acidity of hydrogen halides increases down the group because the H-X bond strength decreases, making it easier to release H⁺ ion. The bond dissociation enthalpy order is: H-F > H-Cl > H-Br > H-I. Therefore, H-I has the weakest bond and is the strongest acid among hydrohalic acids. Order: HI > HBr > HCl > HF.
```
