**P₄O₆ reacts with cold water to give**

```markdown
P₄O₆ (phosphorus trioxide) reacts with cold water to form phosphorus acid (H₃PO₃). P₄O₆ + 6H₂O → 4H₃PO₃. P₄O₁₀ (phosphorus pentoxide) reacts with hot water to give H₃PO₄. H₄P₂O₇ and HPO₃ are pyrophosphoric and metaphosphoric acids respectively.
```
