**Which is true regarding nitrogen?**

```markdown
Nitrogen (N₂) has a strong triple bond (N≡N) with pπ-pπ bonding. Nitrogen is highly electronegative (3.04), has higher ionisation enthalpy than oxygen (since it has half-filled 2p³ stable configuration), and does not have d-orbitals (being a 2nd period element). Therefore, the correct statement is its ability to form pπ-pπ bonds with itself.
```
