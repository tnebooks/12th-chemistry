**On hydrolysis, PCl₃ gives**

```markdown
PCl₃ (phosphorus trichloride) undergoes hydrolysis with water to give phosphorus acid (H₃PO₃) and HCl. PCl₃ + 3H₂O → H₃PO₃ + 3HCl. PH₃ is not formed from PCl₃ hydrolysis. H₃PO₄ is formed from PCl₅ hydrolysis. POCl₃ is formed by partial hydrolysis or reaction with oxygen.
```
