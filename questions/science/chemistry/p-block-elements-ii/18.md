**When copper is heated with conc HNO₃ it produces**

```markdown
Copper reacts with concentrated nitric acid to produce copper nitrate (Cu(NO₃)₂) and nitrogen dioxide (NO₂) gas, along with water. Cu + 4HNO₃(conc.) → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2NO₂ + 2H₂O. NO₂ is a reddish-brown gas. With dilute HNO₃, NO gas is produced. N₂O is produced with very dilute HNO₃ and metals like Zn.
```
