**Which one of the following compounds is not formed?**

```markdown
Neon (Ne) is a noble gas with very high ionization enthalpy (2081 kJ/mol). It cannot form chemical compounds like NeF₂ because its electrons are very tightly held. Xenon compounds like XeOF₄, XeO₃, and XeF₂ are well-known stable compounds. Therefore, NeF₂ is not formed.
```
