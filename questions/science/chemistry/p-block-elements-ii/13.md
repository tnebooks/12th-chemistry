**Most easily liquefiable gas is**

```markdown
Liquefaction depends on the strength of van der Waals forces, which increases down the group with increasing atomic size. The boiling point order of noble gases is: He < Ne < Ar < Kr < Xe < Rn. Higher boiling point means easier liquefaction. Among the given options, Kr has the highest boiling point (-153°C) and is therefore most easily liquefiable.
```
