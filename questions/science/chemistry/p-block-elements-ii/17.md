**Among the following the correct order of acidity is (NEET)**

```markdown
The acidity of oxoacids of chlorine increases with an increase in the number of oxygen atoms attached to chlorine. More oxygen atoms increase the electronegativity of the central atom and stabilize the conjugate base. The order of acidity is: HClO < HClO₂ < HClO₃ < HClO₄. HClO₄ is the strongest acid due to four oxygen atoms (maximum oxidation state of Cl is +7).
```
