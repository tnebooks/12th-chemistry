**The correct order of the thermal stability of hydrogen halide is**

```markdown
Thermal stability decreases as bond strength decreases down the group. H-F bond is the strongest (574 kJ/mol) due to small size and high electronegativity difference. Bond strength order: H-F > H-Cl (431) > H-Br (366) > H-I (299). Therefore, thermal stability order is: HF > HCl > HBr > HI.
```
