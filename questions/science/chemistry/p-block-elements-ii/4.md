**Solid (A) reacts with strong aqueous NaOH liberating a foul smelling gas (B) which spontaneously burn in air giving smoky rings. A and B are respectively**

```markdown
White phosphorus (P₄) reacts with dilute NaOH to produce PH₃ (phosphine) which has a foul fishy smell. PH₃ spontaneously ignites in air forming smoky rings of P₂O₅. Reaction: P₄ + 3NaOH + 3H₂O → PH₃ + 3NaH₂PO₂. Red phosphorus does not react this way. S₈ gives H₂S which does not give smoky rings. Therefore, A = P₄ (white), B = PH₃.
```
