**Among the following, which is the strongest oxidizing agent?**

```markdown
Fluorine (F₂) is the strongest oxidizing agent among halogens because it has the highest electronegativity (4.0) and highest standard reduction potential. It can oxidize all other halide ions to halogens. The oxidizing power order of halogens is: F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂.
```
