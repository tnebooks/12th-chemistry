**XeF₆ on complete hydrolysis produces**

```markdown
XeF₆ undergoes complete hydrolysis to give xenon trioxide (XeO₃) and HF. XeF₆ + 3H₂O → XeO₃ + 6HF. XeOF₄ and XeO₂F₂ are products of partial hydrolysis, not complete hydrolysis. XeO₃ is a colorless, explosive solid.
```
