**The basicity of pyrophosphorous acid (H₄P₂O₅) is**

```markdown
H₄P₂O₅ (pyrophosphorous acid) has 4 hydrogen atoms, but only the hydrogens attached to oxygen atoms (P-OH) are ionizable and contribute to basicity. In this acid, only 2 P-OH groups are present, while the remaining 2 hydrogens are directly attached to phosphorus (P-H), which are not acidic. Thus, basicity is 2.
```
