**Which one of the following orders is correct for the bond dissociation enthalpy of halogen molecules? (NEET)**

```markdown
The bond dissociation enthalpy order of halogen molecules is: Cl₂ (243 kJ/mol) > Br₂ (193 kJ/mol) > F₂ (158 kJ/mol) > I₂ (151 kJ/mol). F₂ has unusually low bond dissociation enthalpy due to lone pair-lone pair repulsion in the small F₂ molecule. Therefore the correct order is: Cl₂ > Br₂ > F₂ > I₂.
```
