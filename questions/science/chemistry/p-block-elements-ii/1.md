**In which of the following, NH₃ is not used?**

```markdown
NH₃ is used in the analysis of group III radicals (Al³⁺, Fe³⁺, Cr³⁺) as a precipitating agent, in group IV radicals (Zn²⁺, Ni²⁺, Co²⁺, Mn²⁺) as a complexing agent, and in Tollen's reagent for silver mirror test. However, Nessler's reagent (K₂HgI₄) is used to detect NH₃ itself, not to prepare it. Therefore, NH₃ is not used in Nessler's reagent.
```
