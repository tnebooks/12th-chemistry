**Crystal field stabilization energy for high spin d⁵ octahedral complex is**

```markdown
For high spin d⁵ configuration: t₂g³ e_g². CFSE = [3 × (-0.4) + 2 × (0.6)]Δ₀ = [-1.2 + 1.2]Δ₀ = 0. The 3 electrons in t₂g contribute -1.2Δ₀ and 2 electrons in e_g contribute +1.2Δ₀. They cancel out, giving CFSE = 0.
```
