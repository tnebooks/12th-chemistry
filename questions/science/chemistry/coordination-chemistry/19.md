**Fac-mer isomerism is shown by**

```markdown
Fac-mer isomerism is shown by octahedral complexes of type [MA₃B₃]. [Co(NH₃)₃(Cl)₃] has 3 NH₃ and 3 Cl⁻ ligands. It shows fac (three identical ligands on one face) and mer (three identical ligands in a meridional plane) isomers. [Co(en)₃]³⁺ shows optical isomerism. [Co(NH₃)₄(Cl)₂]⁺ shows geometrical isomerism. [Co(NH₃)₅Cl]SO₄ shows ionization isomerism.
```
