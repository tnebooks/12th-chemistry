**A magnetic moment of 1.73BM will be shown by one among the following (NEET)**

```markdown
Ti⁴⁺ (d⁰ → 0 BM), Co²⁺ (d⁷ high spin → n=3, μ=√(3×5)=√15=3.87 BM), Cu²⁺ (d⁹ → n=1, μ=√(1×3)=√3=1.732 BM), Ni²⁺ in [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻ (d⁸ low spin, square planar → n=0, μ=0 BM). Therefore [Cu(NH₃)₄]²⁺ has magnetic moment 1.732 BM.
```
