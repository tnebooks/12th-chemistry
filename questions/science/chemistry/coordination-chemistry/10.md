**Which one of the following will give a pair of enantiomorphs?**

```markdown
[Co(en)₂Cl₂]Cl will give optical isomers (enantiomorphs) because en is a chiral bidentate ligand. The complex has no plane of symmetry. [Cr(NH₃)₆][Co(CN)₆] and [Pt(NH₃)₄][PtCl₄] are coordination isomers and optically inactive. [Co(NH₃)₄Cl₂]NO₂ shows geometrical and ionization isomerism but not optical.
```
