**An excess of silver nitrate is added to 100ml of a 0.01M solution of pentaquachloridochromium(III)chloride. The number of moles of AgCl precipitated would be**

```markdown
The complex is [Cr(H₂O)₅Cl]Cl₂. In aqueous solution, it gives 2 Cl⁻ ions outside the coordination sphere. 1000 ml of 1M solution gives 2 moles of Cl⁻ ions. For 100 ml of 0.01M solution: Moles of Cl⁻ = (100 × 0.01 × 2) / 1000 = 0.002 moles. Ag⁺ + Cl⁻ → AgCl, so 0.002 moles of AgCl will be precipitated.
```
