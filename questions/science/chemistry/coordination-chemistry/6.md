**IUPAC name of the complex K₃[Al(C₂O₄)₃] is**

```markdown
The complex is an anionic complex. Potassium is named first. Ligand is oxalato (C₂O₄²⁻) - three ligands so "trioxalato". Al is the central metal; in anionic complex it becomes "aluminate". Al oxidation state: 3K⁺ = +3, 3 oxalato = -6, so Al = +3. Therefore IUPAC name: Potassium trioxalatoaluminate(III).
```
