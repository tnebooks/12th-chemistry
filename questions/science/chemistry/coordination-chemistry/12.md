**How many geometrical isomers are possible for [Pt(Py)(NH₃)(Br)(Cl)]?**

```markdown
[Pt(Py)(NH₃)(Br)(Cl)] is of the type [MABCD] (square planar with four different ligands). For [MABCD] type square planar complexes, 3 geometrical isomers are possible. Taking any one ligand as reference (e.g., Py), the other three ligands can be arranged in 3 different ways around the metal.
```
