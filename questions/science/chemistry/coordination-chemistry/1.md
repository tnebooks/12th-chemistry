**The sum of primary valence and secondary valence of the metal M in the complex [M(en)₂(Ox)]Cl is**

```markdown
The central metal ion is M³⁺ (from Cl⁻ outside the coordination sphere). Primary valence (oxidation state) = +3. Secondary valence (coordination number) = 6 (en is bidentate - contributes 2, Ox is bidentate - contributes 2, Cl is monodentate - contributes 1; total = 2+2+1 = 5? Actually en contributes 4 coordination sites (2 en × 2), Ox contributes 2, Cl contributes 1; total = 4+2+1 = 7? Wait: en is bidentate, so 2 en = 4 sites; Ox is bidentate = 2 sites; Cl is monodentate = 1 site; total = 7. But the correct coordination number for this complex is 6 (en₂ = 4, Ox = 2; Cl is outside the sphere). So secondary valence = 6. Sum = 3 + 6 = 9.
```
