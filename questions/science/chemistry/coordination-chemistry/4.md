**Oxidation state of Iron and the charge on the ligand NO in [Fe(H₂O)₅NO]SO₄ are**

```markdown
In [Fe(H₂O)₅NO]SO₄, SO₄²⁻ is outside. The complex is neutral. NO is considered as NO⁺ (nitrosonium) in this complex. Let Fe be x. x + 0 (5H₂O) + (+1 from NO⁺) = +2 (to balance SO₄²⁻). So x + 1 = +2 → x = +1. Therefore Fe is +1 and NO is +1. This is the brown ring complex where Fe is in +1 oxidation state.
```
