**Formula of tris(ethane-1,2-diamine)iron(II)phosphate**

```markdown
en (ethane-1,2-diamine) is H₂N-CH₂-CH₂-NH₂, a neutral bidentate ligand. Fe²⁺ has charge +2. Phosphate PO₄³⁻ has charge -3. For neutral compound: 3 Fe²⁺ + 2 PO₄³⁻ gives Fe₃(PO₄)₂. For one Fe²⁺ ion: [Fe(en)₃]²⁺ and PO₄³⁻ → [Fe(en)₃](PO₄) = charge +2-2=0? Actually Fe(en)₃²⁺ has +2 charge, needs PO₄³⁻ (charge -3) - not balanced. 3[Fe(en)₃]²⁺ + 2PO₄³⁻ → [Fe(en)₃]₃(PO₄)₂. So formula: [Fe(H₂N-CH₂-CH₂-NH₂)₃](PO₄)₂? No - 3 Fe²⁺ and 2 PO₄³⁻ gives net 0. So [Fe(en)₃]₃(PO₄)₂. But option (d) is [Fe(en)₃](PO₄)₂ with Fe²⁺? Wait: 3 Fe²⁺ + 2 PO₄³⁻ = 0. So it is [Fe(en)₃]₃(PO₄)₂. Option (d) is correct.
```
