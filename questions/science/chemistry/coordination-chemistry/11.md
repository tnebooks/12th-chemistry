**Which type of isomerism is exhibited by [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂]?**

```markdown
[Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] is a square planar complex of type [MA₂B₂]. It exhibits geometrical isomerism: cis (both NH₃ adjacent, both Cl adjacent) and trans (NH₃ opposite, Cl opposite). It does not show optical isomerism due to presence of plane of symmetry.
```
