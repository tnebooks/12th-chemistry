**Which of the following is paramagnetic in nature?**

```markdown
[Zn(NH₃)₄]²⁺ - Zn²⁺ (d¹⁰) - diamagnetic (no unpaired electrons). [Co(NH₃)₆]³⁺ - Co³⁺ (d⁶) low spin - t₂g⁶ e_g⁰ - diamagnetic. [Ni(H₂O)₆]²⁺ - Ni²⁺ (d⁸) high spin - 2 unpaired electrons - paramagnetic. [Ni(CN)₄]²⁻ - Ni²⁺ (d⁸) low spin, square planar - diamagnetic.
```
