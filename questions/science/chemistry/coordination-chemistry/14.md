**Which kind of isomerism is possible for a complex [Co(NH₃)₄Br₂]Cl?**

```markdown
[Co(NH₃)₄Br₂]Cl is of type [MA₄B₂] which shows geometrical isomerism (cis and trans). It also shows ionization isomerism: [Co(NH₃)₄BrCl]Br (where Br is inside the sphere). It does not show optical isomerism because [MA₄B₂] type has a plane of symmetry.
```
