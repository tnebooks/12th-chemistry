**Which one of the following complexes is not expected to exhibit isomerism?**

```markdown
[FeCl₆]³⁻ has all identical ligands (six Cl⁻). It cannot show any type of isomerism. [Ni(NH₃)₄(H₂O)₂]²⁺ shows geometrical isomerism. [Pt(NH₃)₂Cl₂] shows geometrical isomerism. [Co(NH₃)₅SO₄]Cl shows ionization isomerism.
```
