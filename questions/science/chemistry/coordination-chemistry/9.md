**In which of the following coordination entities the magnitude of Δ₀ will be maximum?**

```markdown
In all complexes, the central metal ion is Co³⁺. The magnitude of Δ₀ depends on the ligand strength. According to the spectrochemical series: I⁻ < Br⁻ < Cl⁻ < F⁻ < OH⁻ < H₂O < NH₃ < en < CN⁻ < CO. CN⁻ (cyanide) is the strongest ligand among the given options. Therefore [Co(CN)₆]³⁻ will have the maximum Δ₀ value.
```
