In the Ellingham diagram, for the formation of carbon monoxide

```markdown
The formation of CO has a negative slope in the Ellingham diagram because 2C + O₂ → 2CO is an entropy-increasing reaction (ΔS⁰ is positive). Since ΔG⁰ = ΔH⁰ - TΔS⁰, if ΔS⁰ is positive, as T increases, the term -TΔS⁰ becomes more negative, making ΔG⁰ more negative. Therefore, the slope (ΔG⁰/ΔT) is negative. CO becomes more stable at higher temperatures.
```
