Which of the following is used for concentrating ore in metallurgy?

```markdown
Concentration of ore (dressing of ore) involves removal of gangue. Leaching (chemical method) is used for concentration of ores like bauxite (Baeyer's process), gold/Ag (cyanide process). Froth floatation is used for sulphide ores. Roasting is a conversion process (not concentration) where ore is heated in air. Therefore, both (a) leaching and (c) froth floatation are used for concentration.
```
