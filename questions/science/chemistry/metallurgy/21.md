Which of the following plot gives Ellingham diagram?

```markdown
Ellingham diagram is a plot of Gibbs free energy change (ΔG⁰) versus temperature (T) for the formation of metal oxides from their corresponding elements. It is a graphical representation that helps predict the feasibility of reduction of metal oxides. The slope of the line gives -ΔS⁰ (since ΔG⁰ = ΔH⁰ - TΔS⁰, a plot of ΔG⁰ vs T gives a straight line with slope -ΔS⁰).
```
