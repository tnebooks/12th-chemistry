In the extraction of aluminium from alumina by electrolysis, cryolite is added to

```markdown
Pure alumina (Al₂O₃) has a very high melting point (~2050°C), making electrolysis impractical and expensive. Cryolite (Na₃AlF₆) is added to lower the melting point of alumina to about 950°C. It also increases the electrical conductivity of the mixture. Cryolite does not remove impurities or increase the rate of reduction directly.
```
