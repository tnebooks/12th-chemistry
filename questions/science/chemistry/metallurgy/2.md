Roasting of sulphide ore gives the gas (A). (A) is a colourless gas. Aqueous solution of (A) is acidic. The gas (A) is

```markdown
Roasting of sulphide ores (like ZnS, PbS, Cu₂S) involves heating in excess air. The reaction is: 2ZnS + 3O₂ → 2ZnO + 2SO₂. SO₂ is a colourless gas with a pungent smell. When dissolved in water, it forms sulphurous acid (H₂SO₃), which is acidic. CO₂ is formed from carbonates, SO₃ is formed by further oxidation of SO₂, and H₂S has a rotten egg smell and is not formed during roasting.
```
