Flux is a substance which is used to convert

```markdown
Flux is a substance added to the ore during smelting to remove gangue (impurities). It converts infusible (non-melting) impurities into fusible (melting) and soluble slag. Acidic flux (SiO₂) removes basic impurities like CaO, MgO. Basic flux (CaO) removes acidic impurities like SiO₂. Thus, flux converts infusible impurities to soluble impurities.
```
