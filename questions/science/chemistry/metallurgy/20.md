In the electrolytic refining of copper, which one of the following is used as anode?

```markdown
In the electrolytic refining of copper, impure copper is used as the anode and pure copper is used as the cathode. The electrolyte is acidified copper sulphate (CuSO₄ + H₂SO₄). On passing current, impure copper dissolves at the anode and pure copper deposits at the cathode. Impurities settle at the bottom as anode mud. Carbon rod and platinum electrode are used in other electrochemical processes.
```
