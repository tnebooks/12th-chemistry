Which of the following is not true with respect to Ellingham diagram?

```markdown
The graph for the formation of CO₂ (C + O₂ → CO₂) is NOT parallel to the free energy axis. It has a small negative slope. It is almost horizontal (slightly negative) because ΔS⁰ for CO₂ formation is small. The graph for CO is the one that is highly sloped (negative slope) and can cross other lines. All other statements are true: phase changes cause deviation (change in slope); CO negative slope means it becomes more stable at higher temperatures; positive slope of metal oxides means their stability decreases with temperature (less negative ΔG⁰).
```
