How many moles of I₂ are liberated when 1 mole of potassium dichromate reacts with potassium iodide?

```markdown
Cr₂O₇²⁻ oxidizes iodide in acidic medium: Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 14H⁺ + 6I⁻ → 2Cr³⁺ + 3I₂ + 7H₂O. Thus 1 mole dichromate liberates 3 moles I₂.
```
