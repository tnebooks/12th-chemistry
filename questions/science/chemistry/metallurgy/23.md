Which of the following reduction is not thermodynamically feasible?

```markdown
Al₂O₃ + 2Cr → Cr₂O₃ + 2Al is not feasible because Al is more reactive than Cr (Al is above Cr in the reactivity series). Al has a greater affinity for oxygen than Cr. Therefore, Al₂O₃ cannot be reduced by Cr. The reverse reaction (Cr₂O₃ + 2Al → Al₂O₃ + 2Cr) is the thermite reaction and is highly feasible. 3TiO₂ + 4Al → 2Al₂O₃ + 3Ti is also feasible because Al reduces TiO₂.
```
