Which one of the following ore is best concentrated by froth-floatation method?


```markdown
Froth floatation method is used for the concentration of sulphide ores because they are preferentially wetted by frothing agents (pine oil) and become attached to the froth. Galena (PbS) is a sulphide ore and is best concentrated by froth floatation. Magnetite (Fe₃O₄) is concentrated by magnetic separation. Haematite (Fe₂O₃) and Cassiterite (SnO₂) are concentrated by gravity separation (washing).
```
