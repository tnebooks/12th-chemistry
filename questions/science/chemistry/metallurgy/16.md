Considering Ellingham diagram, which of the following metals can be used to reduce alumina?


```markdown
In the Ellingham diagram, the metal whose oxide formation line is lower than that of the oxide being reduced can act as a reducing agent. Al₂O₃ is very stable (line is very low). Mg has a lower line than Al₂O₃ at all temperatures, meaning Mg is more reactive and can reduce Al₂O₃: 3Mg + Al₂O₃ → 2Al + 3MgO. Fe, Cu, and Zn have higher lines and cannot reduce Al₂O₃.
```
