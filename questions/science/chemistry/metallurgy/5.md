Which of the metal is extracted by Hall-Heroult process?

```markdown
The Hall-Heroult process is used for the extraction of aluminium from purified alumina (Al₂O₃). In this process, alumina is dissolved in molten cryolite (Na₃AlF₆) and electrolysed. Aluminium is obtained at the cathode. This process was developed independently by Charles Hall and Paul Héroult in 1886. Ni, Cu, and Zn are extracted by other methods.
```
