Which of the following statements, about the advantage of roasting of sulphide ore before reduction is not true?

```markdown
Carbon and hydrogen are NOT suitable reducing agents for metal sulphides because the Gibbs free energy (ΔGᵣ⁰) for the formation of CS₂ and H₂S is less negative (or more positive) than for metal sulphides. This makes the reduction thermodynamically unfavourable. The other statements are true: roasting has negative ΔGᵣ⁰, is thermodynamically feasible, and converts sulphide to oxide which is easier to reduce.
```
