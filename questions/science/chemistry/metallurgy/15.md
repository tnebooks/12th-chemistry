Extraction of gold and silver involves leaching with cyanide ion. Silver is later recovered by

```markdown
Gold and silver are leached with dilute NaCN or KCN in the presence of air. The complex formed is [Ag(CN)₂]⁻ or [Au(CN)₂]⁻. Silver is recovered from the solution by displacement with zinc (Merrill-Crowe process): 2[Ag(CN)₂]⁻ + Zn → [Zn(CN)₄]²⁻ + 2Ag↓. Zinc is more reactive than silver and displaces it from the complex.
```
