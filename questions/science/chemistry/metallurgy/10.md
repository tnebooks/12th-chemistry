```markdown
Electrochemical process (electrolysis of molten salts) is used to extract highly reactive metals like sodium, potassium, magnesium, calcium, and aluminium. Sodium is extracted by the Down's process (electrolysis of molten NaCl). Iron is extracted by blast furnace reduction. Lead is extracted by carbon reduction. Silver is extracted by cyanide process.
```
