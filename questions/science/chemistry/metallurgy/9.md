Which one of the following is not feasible?

```markdown
Copper is less reactive than zinc (Cu is below Zn in the electrochemical series). Therefore, Cu cannot displace Zn from Zn²⁺ solution. The reaction Cu(s) + Zn²⁺(aq) → Zn(s) + Cu²⁺(aq) is not feasible. All other reactions are feasible: Zn displaces Cu, Cu displaces Ag, and Fe displaces Cu because the reducing agent is more reactive than the metal being displaced.
```
