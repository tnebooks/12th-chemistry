Bauxite has the composition

```markdown
Bauxite is the principal ore of aluminium. Its chemical composition is Al₂O₃·nH₂O (hydrated aluminium oxide), where n varies from 1 to 3. It is not pure Al₂O₃; it contains impurities like Fe₂O₃, SiO₂, and TiO₂. Fe₂O₃·2H₂O is the composition of limonite (iron ore). Therefore, bauxite has the composition Al₂O₃·nH₂O.
```
