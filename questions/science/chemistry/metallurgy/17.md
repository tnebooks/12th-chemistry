The following set of reactions are used in refining Zirconium:

Zr (impure) + 2I2 → ZrI4

ZrI4 → Zr (pure) + 2I2

This method is known as

```markdown
The Van Arkel process (also called Van Arkel-de Boer process) is used for refining metals like Zr, Ti, Hf, etc. The impure metal is reacted with iodine at lower temperature to form volatile metal iodide. The metal iodide is then decomposed at higher temperature on a hot filament to deposit pure metal. Mond's process is for nickel (Ni + 4CO → Ni(CO)₄ → Ni + 4CO).
```
