During the decomposition of H₂O₂ to give dioxygen, 48 g O₂ is formed per minute at a certain point of time. The rate of formation of water at this point is:

```markdown
For 2H₂O₂ → 2H₂O + O₂, 48 g O₂ = 48/32 = 1.5 mol O₂ per minute. Water forms at twice this rate, so 3.0 mol min⁻¹.
```
