The incorrect statement among the following is

```markdown
In the metallurgy of gold, the metal is leached with dilute sodium cyanide solution (NaCN), NOT sodium chloride (NaCl). The reaction is: 4Au + 8CN⁻ + 2H₂O + O₂ → 4[Au(CN)₂]⁻ + 4OH⁻. Nickel is refined by Mond's process (Ni + 4CO → Ni(CO)₄). Titanium is refined by Van Arkel process. Zinc blende (ZnS) is a sulphide ore concentrated by froth floatation.
```
