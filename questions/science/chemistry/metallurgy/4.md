The metal oxide which cannot be reduced to metal by carbon is


```markdown
Al₂O₃ cannot be reduced by carbon because aluminium has a very high affinity for oxygen (Al is above carbon in the reactivity series). The reduction of Al₂O₃ by carbon would require very high temperatures (>2000°C) and would form Al₄C₃ (aluminium carbide) instead of Al metal. PbO, ZnO, and FeO can be reduced by carbon (Coke) as they are less reactive.
```
