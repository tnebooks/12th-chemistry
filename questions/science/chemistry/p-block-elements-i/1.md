**An aqueous solution of borax is**

```markdown
When borax (Na₂B₄O₇) dissolves in water, it undergoes hydrolysis: Na₂B₄O₇ + 7H₂O → 2NaOH + 4H₃BO₃. A strong base (NaOH) and a weak acid (H₃BO₃) are formed. Since the base is stronger than the acid, the aqueous solution is basic in nature.
```
