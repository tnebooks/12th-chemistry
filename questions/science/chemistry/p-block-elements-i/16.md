**Duralumin is an alloy of**

```markdown
Duralumin is an aluminium-based alloy developed in 1909 by Alfred Wilm. Its composition is: Aluminium - 95%, Copper - 4%, Manganese - 0.5%, Magnesium - 0.5%. It is a strong and lightweight alloy that can be heat-treated. It is widely used in aircraft structures, automotive parts, and transportation industries due to its excellent strength-to-weight ratio.
```
