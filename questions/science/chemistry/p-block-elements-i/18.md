**The stability of +1 oxidation state increases in the sequence**

```markdown
In group 13 elements (Al, Ga, In, Tl), as we move down the group, the stability of the +1 oxidation state increases. This is due to the "inert pair effect" - the reluctance of the ns² electrons to participate in bonding. Tl⁺ is the most stable among these. Therefore, the correct order of increasing stability of +1 oxidation state is: Al < Ga < In < Tl. This is why Tl commonly shows +1 oxidation state while Al shows +3.
```
