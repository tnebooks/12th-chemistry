**In diborane, the number of electrons that accounts for banana bonds is**

```markdown
Diborane (B₂H₆) contains two 3-center-2-electron (3c-2e) banana bonds (bridge bonds). Each 3c-2e bond involves 2 electrons. Therefore, the total number of electrons involved in both bridge bonds is 2 × 2 = 4 electrons.
```
