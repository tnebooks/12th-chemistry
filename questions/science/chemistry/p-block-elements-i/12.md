**Which of the following is not sp² hybridised?**

```markdown
Dry ice is solid CO₂. In CO₂, carbon is sp hybridised with a linear structure (O=C=O). Graphite, Graphene, and Fullerene (C₆₀) all have carbon atoms in sp² hybridisation state with trigonal planar geometry. Dry ice has carbon in sp hybridisation, not sp².
```
