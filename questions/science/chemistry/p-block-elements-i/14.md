**Which of the following statements is not correct?**

```markdown
Beryl (Be₃Al₂Si₆O₁₈) is indeed a cyclic silicate with Si₆O₁₈ rings. Mg₂SiO₄ (forsterite) is an orthosilicate with isolated [SiO₄]⁴⁻ units. [SiO₄]⁴⁻ is the basic structural unit of all silicates. Feldspar is an aluminosilicate with a three-dimensional framework, but the statement "Feldspar is a three dimensional silicate" is incomplete/incorrect because it should specifically say "aluminosilicate" as it contains Al replacing some Si atoms.
```
