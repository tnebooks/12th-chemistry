**Carbon atoms in fullerene with formula C₆₀ have**

```markdown
C₆₀ (Buckminsterfullerene) has a cage-like structure with 12 pentagonal and 20 hexagonal rings. Each carbon atom is bonded to three other carbon atoms and has a planar/trigonal geometry. Therefore, each carbon atom is sp² hybridised.
```
