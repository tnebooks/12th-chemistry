**Which among the following is not a borane?**
```markdown
Boranes generally follow the formulae BₙHₙ₊₄ (nido boranes) and BₙHₙ₊₆ (arachno boranes). B₃H₆ does not fit either formula. B₂H₆ (diborane) and B₄H₁₀ (tetraborane) are well-known boranes. B₃H₆ is an unstable/non-existent compound, hence it is not a borane.
```
