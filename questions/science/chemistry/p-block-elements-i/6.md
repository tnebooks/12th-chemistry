**The element that does not show catenation among the following p-block elements is**

```markdown
Catenation is the ability of an element to form bonds with itself, forming chains or rings. Carbon shows maximum catenation, followed by Silicon and Germanium. However, Lead (Pb) has very poor catenation property because the Pb-Pb bond is very weak due to poor orbital overlap and inert pair effect.
```
