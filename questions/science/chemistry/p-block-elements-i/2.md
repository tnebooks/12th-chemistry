**Boric acid is an acid because its molecule (NEET)**

```markdown
Boric acid B(OH)₃ does not directly release H⁺ ions. Instead, it accepts OH⁻ from water to form [B(OH)₄]⁻ and releases H⁺. The reaction is: B(OH)₃ + H₂O ⇌ [B(OH)₄]⁻ + H⁺. Thus, it acts as a proton donor (acid) through this mechanism, accepting OH⁻ from water.
```
