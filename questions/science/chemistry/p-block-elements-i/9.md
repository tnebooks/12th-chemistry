**The basic structural unit of silicates is (NEET)**

```markdown
The basic structural unit of silicates is the silicon tetrahedron. One silicon atom is bonded to four oxygen atoms forming [SiO₄]⁴⁻ tetrahedral units. These units combine in various ways (sharing corners, edges, or faces) to form different silicate structures like ortho, pyro, cyclic, chain, sheet, and framework silicates.
```
