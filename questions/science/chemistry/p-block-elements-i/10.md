**The repeating unit in silicone is**

```markdown
Silicones are organosilicon polymers with the general unit R₂SiO, where R is typically methyl (-CH₃) or phenyl (-C₆H₅) groups. They consist of long chains with alternating Si-O-Si bonds. The repeating unit in silicone is SiO₂. The empirical formula is R₂SiO, which corresponds to SiO₂ as the inorganic repeating unit.
```
