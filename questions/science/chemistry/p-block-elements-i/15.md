**Match items in Column-I with Column-II and assign the correct code.**

Column-I:
A) Borazole
B) Boric acid
C) Quartz
D) Borax

Column-II:
1) B(OH)₃
2) B₃N₃H₆
3) Na₂[B₄O₅(OH)₄]·8H₂O
4) SiO₂

```markdown
Borazole is also known as inorganic benzene with formula B₃N₃H₆ (Match A-2). Boric acid has the formula B(OH)₃ (Match B-1). Quartz is a crystalline form of SiO₂ (Match C-4). Borax is Na₂[B₄O₅(OH)₄]·8H₂O (Match D-3). Therefore, the correct matching is A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3.
```
