**The compound that is used in nuclear reactors as protective shields and control rods is**

```markdown
Boron has a high neutron absorption capacity due to its large neutron capture cross-section. Metal borides (such as B₄C, boron carbide, and various metal borides like ZB₂, HfB₂) are used as protective shields and control rods in nuclear reactors. They absorb neutrons and help control the nuclear fission chain reaction without becoming radioactive themselves.
```
