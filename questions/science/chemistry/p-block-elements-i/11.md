**Which of these is not a monomer for a high molecular mass silicone polymer?**

```markdown
Silicone polymers require bifunctional monomers for chain formation. Me₃SiCl (trimethylsilyl chloride) is monofunctional and acts only as a chain-ending unit, not as a monomer for high molecular mass polymers. Me₂SiCl₂ (bifunctional) forms linear chains, while MeSiCl₃ and PhSiCl₃ (trifunctional) form cross-linked or network polymers.
```
