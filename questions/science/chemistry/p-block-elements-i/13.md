**The geometry at which carbon atoms in diamond are bonded to each other is**

```markdown
In diamond, each carbon atom is sp³ hybridised. As a result, each carbon atom is bonded to four other carbon atoms through sigma bonds. The geometry around each carbon atom is tetrahedral with a bond angle of 109.5°. This gives diamond a very strong three-dimensional network structure.
```
