**Oxidation state of carbon in its hydrides**

```markdown
In carbon hydrides like CH₄ (methane), hydrogen has an oxidation state of +1. Since the molecule is neutral, the sum of oxidation states must be zero. Let x be the oxidation state of carbon: x + 4(+1) = 0 → x = -4. Thus, carbon exhibits -4 oxidation state in its hydrides.
```
