The molar conductivity of a 0.5 mol dm⁻³ solution of AgNO₃ with specific conductance 5.76 × 10⁻³ S cm⁻¹ at 298 K is:

```markdown
Λm = κ × 1000/C = (5.76 × 10⁻³ × 1000)/0.5 = 11.52 S cm² mol⁻¹.
```
